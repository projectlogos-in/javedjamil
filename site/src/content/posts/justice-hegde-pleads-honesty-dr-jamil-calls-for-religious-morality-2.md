“Indian secularism does not mean negation of religion but it surely means negation of communalism”, said Dr Javed Jamil, renowned thinker who holds chair at Yenepoya University Mangaluru. He said that communalism has no relationship with religion and is a product of political and economic fundamentalism. He was addressing an Eid get together program at Bangaluru. He said that the Hindutva has nothing to do with Hindu religion but is a movement driven by the forces of economics in the country to sustain their political and economic hegemony. He argued that while in the past Brahmanism and Brahmans dominated the Indian scene, in the last few decades baniyawad and baniyas are virtually ruling the economy. He stressed that Muslims should invite all the religions and religious organizations for an alliance against immorality and dangerous economics. He said that if organizations like RSS want to play a really positive role they must work for religious morality rather than religious identify.

![](https://javedjamilhome.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/09/3d6ce-1lcnbq1tcvb4w693q8lvxzq.png?w=736)  

Former Lokayukta Justice N Santosh Hegde said if one is content, he will never worry about the Income Tax or Directorate of Enforcement raids, taking a dig at the recent raids by these agencies.

“The Lokayukta report on illegal mining had named three CMs and indicted them for corruption. One of them was nominated as the CM candidate. We still accept such things and we also pay the bribe to get our work done. What does this show? Even society has to be blamed for the situation we are facing,”

Hegde said. “I was a frog in the well before I was Lokayukta. I thought everyone’s life was good because my life was good. Later, I realised hardships people face for basic needs. When huge cuts are taken away from amount released for the welfare of people and developmental works, how can a country progress?” he asked.
