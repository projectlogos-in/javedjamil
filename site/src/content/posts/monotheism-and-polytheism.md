#### A Systematic Study of the Holy Qur’an — PART II: PHILOSOPHY

![](https://javedjamilhome.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/09/2183d-1qk6zo8gdlf41gr0xmp7clw.jpeg?w=736)  

S**ince** the earliest days of human history, man has been worshipping diverse objects of nature. He started worshipping whatever he found possessing some form of extraordinary power or energy. So, he worships, even till date, fire, sea, snakes and other animals and a large number of idols; some of which are images of saints, others of angels or _Devtas_; there are still a large number of idols that are only the result of man’s wild fancies. **The question is: whatever their origin, do they deserve worship?**

Whatever is there in the universe has been created by God; and out of His creations, man is undoubtedly the best; for he alone has the capability to think, imagine, invent and produce. If God is the original (primary) creator, man is the secondary creator because he possesses mental and physical capabilities to transform some of God’s original creation into a form suitable for his own use. Having bestowed some of His Own virtues (like wisdom, authority and will), God chose him, and him alone, **His viceroy on the Earth, and ordained that all other creations would be subservient to him**. So, the sun, the moon air, water, clouds, animals and plants — all are directly or indirectly serving the mankind. **It therefore neither behoves man nor satisfies the majesty and Glory of the real Master that a Viceroy, only second in powers to the Sovereign, should worship his own subordinates** that in fact have been put into his service by the command of the Sovereign.

![](https://javedjamilhome.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/09/c068d-11ra5ixwf8rc87kthtqi9aw.png?w=736)  

Those that take others as partners of God are therefore in the gravest error. **If there had been other gods besides one God, the result would have been a great disaster; an all-pervading chaos would have been the order of the day.** Think of a situation where one hundred artists are handed over one and the same canvas to draw their designs on. What will happen? Confusion, trouble and disaster. But when one observes the harmony so beautifully and so perfectly established in the universe, one cannot but conclude that it is truly a Great Work of a Great Artist, a Wonderful Design of a Wonderful Designer, and a Perfect Creation of a Perfect Creator. The minute details of the matter make it clear beyond an iota of doubt that the whole **universe is the Plan of One, and the only One Planner**. Look at the internal structure of anything. Every matter in the universe has electrons and protons as its basic components. There is no single exception to this rule and thus they are following the same laws everywhere. **Similarly, the twentieth century physics categorically declares that “natural laws are the same everywhere in the universe”.** With more than one God, was it just possible?

Monotheism does not only imply rejection of idols (of clay and stone) but **also the false gods of Money, Power, Science and Desire**which human beings have been worshipping or eulogising in distinct violation of the bounds of God. True monotheism requires total and uncompromised surrender to the Will of God. In no matters whatsoever, God’s bounds will be violated; there is no room for pursuing selfish ends outside the limits imposed by Him. When the System demands it, all personal assets should be sacrificed for the sake of the supremacy of His Law, which is aimed at establishing universal peace, justice and brotherhood.

> **In the name of God, the Kind, the Merciful**

### MONOTHEISM VERSUS POLYTHEISM IN THE QUR’AN

#### **Many gods would cause chaos**

Have they held gods from within the Earth that communicate (with them)? Had there been any god in them (Earth and Cosmic Tiers) other than God, both of them would have faced chaos. Subhan (the Engine) belongs to God, the Lord of the Axis and He is beyond what they depict Him as. (21: 21–22)

Have they held deities other than Him? State: “Produce your proof; this is the memorandum for my contemporaries as well as a memorandum for my predecessors.” The fact on the contrary is that most of them are unaware about the Mandate; so they turn away. Moreover, We did not send any Ambassador before you except without having been sent this message through inspiration: There is no god other than I; so do not worship anyone but Me. Further, they say: “The Kind Lord has fathered an offspring.” Subhan (the Engine) is His; they are His honoured subjects. They do not take lead ahead of Him in speech; and they act only in accordance with His Rule. (21: 24–27)

God did not have any son, nor there is any other god along with Him; in that case, every god would have run away with what he created and they would have sought supremacy over one another. Subhan(the Engine) is His, and He is beyond what they depict Him as. (23: 91)

#### God the Only Guide

Ask (them): Out of your associate-gods is there one who can guide you to the Mandate? State: God guides as required by the Mandate; is then God who guides you to the Mandate worthy of being obeyed or one who is in no position to guide unless he is given guidance himself? What is then the issue with you? Do you not have sense? Further, the majority of them follow according to their guesswork; surely, guesswork cannot enrich you a bit the way the Mandate can. Surely, is fully aware of whatever they do. (10: 35–36)

#### Pantheists just follow customs

You not bother about what these people worship; they worship nothing but what their forefathers have been worshipping till now; and there is no doubt that we will pay them what they deserve (against their actions) without any cuts; (11: 109)

#### No Partners of God

God gives the example of an employee under a master; he does not have any authority over anything; and there is another one whom We have supplied best resources from Our side; so he expends out of it (as he wishes), privately as well as publicly. Can the two be equal? The Acknowledgment is for God; but the majority of them do not understand this. God gives another example of two men. One of them is dumb with hardly any capability, and is a burden on his master; whatever he directs him to do, he does not do anything useful. Can such a man be equated with a person who acts justly and treads an established course? (16: 75–76)

(You have to be) true to God and never include anyone else in worship other than Him; if anyone includes others in worship along with God, it is as if one has fallen from the sky and has either been seized by birds or flung by winds to a remote place. (22: 31)

Mankind! A comparison is given; so listen to it! Surely, the deities you invoke in place of God cannot create even a fly, even if they all combine for that purpose. Further, if a fly takes away anything from their own self, they cannot even get it released. Weak indeed are these seekers as well as the sought. (22: 73)

Do they really include in worship other deities (along with God) that cannot create anything but are created themselves? Further, they are neither in a position to help them nor in a position to help themselves. If you invite these people to the Guidance, they will not follow you: it is the same whether you call them or remain silent. Certainly, the deities you invoke instead of God are His subjects the same way as you are. So, if you think you are right, invoke them and let them attend to your prayers! Do they have feet to walk on, or hands to strike with, or eyes to see, or ears to hear? So, tell them: “invite all these extra-gods and then conspire against me and then do not give me any time (to respond).” God is my caretaker indeed; and he always takes care of the good people. (7: 191–196)

#### Frail masters

The comparison of the people who hold gods other than God as their caretaker is with the spider making a nest for itself; and certainly the spider’s nest is among the flimsiest of all the homes; one wishes they knew it! (29: 41)

God puts forth a Parable a man belonging to many partners at variance with each other, and a man belonging entirely to one master: are those two equal in comparison? Praise be to God. But most of them have no knowledge. (39: 29/A)

God strikes a parallel of a man working for several partners always having differences with one another and a man working for a single man; are the two equal in comparison? The Acknowledgment is for God; but the majority of them do not understand this. (39: 29)

#### Mere fancies

Moreover, out of the crops and the cattle God produced for them in abundance, they would fix a share for Him, and would proudly say “This is for God and the rest for our extra-gods.” Following this, (not only) the share meant for their extra-gods is not delivered to God, but also whatever they fix for God is delivered (not to God but to) to the extra-gods. What they act upon is truly bad. (6: 136)

And the people who invoke extra-gods in place of Him do not follow anything but their wishful thinking, and they are simply spreading falsehood. (10: 66)

Similarly, their extra-gods have made the sacrifice of their children an attractive proposition for the purpose of their destruction with their own hands and creating confusion in their minds about their religious system. If God had not given them freedom (of action), they would not have done it. So (you also) ignore them and the distortions they make. (6: 137)

#### True Lord

State: Surely my Lord has indeed guided me to the Established Path, the straightforward system in the continuous way of Abraham the truthful; and he was not among the worshippers of extra-gods.” State (further): “Undoubtedly, my prayer, my service of sacrifice, my life and my death, all are solely for God, the Lord of the Metaverse. He has no co-sharer; and this is what I have been ordained; and of course I am the first among Muslims.” State (furthermore): “Should I seek any lord for me other than God when He is Lord of everything.” (6: 161–164)

And (there will come) a day when He will call them and will then ask: “Where are My co-sharers that you used to be proud of?” The persons on whom the statement of truth was mandated will say: “Our Lord! They are the people who we misguided; we misguided them only because we were misguided ourselves; we dissociate ourselves from them and come to you; it was not we (but extra-gods) they used to worship.” Subsequently, it will be said to them: “implore your extra-gods”; they will implore them but their (extra-gods) will not listen to their (prayers); and they will then see the Punishment (in front of their eyes); (How one wishes) they became rightly guided! (28: 62–64)

Ask them: Do you visualise this? If God keeps the night incessantly placed on you till the Day of Halt, which deity other than God can provide the light to you? Will you then not listen? Ask again: Do you visualise this? If God keeps the day incessantly placed on you, which deity other than God can make the night available to you for resting? (28: 71–72)

It is God who created you; then he provided you the resources (for your living); following that He causes you to die and will give you a new life again. Can your extra-gods perform any of these functions? Subhan (the Engine) is His, and he is beyond the reach of the extra-gods they worship. (30: 40)

What! Do they worship extra-gods who have ordained on them a certain part of the system without the signal from God? Had a decision of the deferred judgment not already been taken, the matter with them would have been finished by now. And certainly the offenders will receive a dreadful punishment. (42: 21)
