#### **Report by Bari Masood**

Renowned Islamic Scholar and thinker, Dr. Javed Jamil has called for **“total ideological onslaught” against Falsehood and false ideologies including Westernism.** He was invited to speak at the Plenary Session of Jamiat Ulama-i-Hind’s two day 33rd general session. Speaking to a gathering of more than 10000 Ulama from all over the country on Saturday, he said that **“we can no longer continue to have a defensive approach and have to challenge the false ideologies of the so-called modernity head-on through ideological counter-attack.”** He said that the **Qur’anic verses beautifully describe “the harmony present in the universe” which exists due to the “unfailing obedience of the galaxies, stars, planets and all other universal particles towards God” and that “the chaos on the earth is the result of the mankind adopting false ideologies instead of the System of God.”** He lambasted the forces of modernity for following “Economic Fundamentalism” and “commercialising all human weaknesses” for the sake of wealth. He said that “wealth has become the biggest god for the forces of modernity” and they want the mankind to follow their diktats in the name of “freedom of Choice”. He told that **more than 7 crore (70 million) people die worldwide due to indulging in practices declared Haram by Islam including “50 million deaths of children in the wombs of their mothers” just because their parents want to “enjoy rights without responsibilities”.**

![](https://javedjamilhome.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/09/d39e8-12gdnutmokkillinp0hrgbw.png?w=736)  

Dr. Javed Jamil lambasted those who keep on exclusively targeting Muslims for terrorism without “condemning the biggest killers of humanity”. He said that the alleged mastermind of 9/11 attacks, Osama Ben Laden was killed 11 years in an isolated operation in Pakistan but **nobody questioned America and its allies “why they killed 35 lakh (3.5 million) innocent Muslims in Iraq and Afghanistan.”** **In the midst of a thundering applause from the crowd, he demanded that “if we have to condemn Muslim terrorists a few thousand times for a few thousand deaths of non-Muslims, we will have to condemn America and other anti-Muslim forces 35 lakh (3.5 million) times for killing 3.5 million innocent Muslims.”** He argued that “each and every single innocent death must be condemned irrespective of the identity of the perpetrator, the identity of the victim, the place, motive and timing of the attack and the method of attack**”. He told that in India Terrorism is more of a Hindu rather than Muslim monopoly alleged by the anti-Muslim forces. He informed the gathering that in last 3 decades more than 41000 deaths have occurred on account of terrorist attacks in India and out of these Muslims are allegedly involved for only 1400 killings. He asked who the Naxalites, Ulfa, Bodo and Tamils are who have killed tens of thousands in India and more than one hundred thousand in Sri Lanka.**

![](https://javedjamilhome.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/09/f82b7-13tmo8xic3kquksysspcmhg.png?w=736)  

**Speaking in favour of resolution on Riots, he told that about one hundred thousand people have lost lives in communal riots since Independence and more than 75 percent of these were Muslims. This proves that the riots in India are primarily the killers of Muslims than anyone else. There is no reason why the Targeted Communal Violence Act should be passed without any delay.**

Dr. Javed Jamil called upon Ulama to take upon the responsibility of leading the mankind. **“But for this, they will have to understand the true nature and stratagems of the prevailing ideologies and forces”,** he said. He argued that Deen **cannot be properly understood and applied without properly understanding the nature of Dunya.”**

Dr. Jamil praised Ulama for their devotion and said that the masses must follow them and must take their guidance in all their affairs. He supported the resolutions presented in the Session. Before his address, Maulana Mahmood Madani, Secretary General and the inspiration behind the Ijlaas, introduced Dr. Javed Jamil as a “thinker who has done commendable studies on Qur’an and has brought numerous thought-provoking ideas to the attention of the intellectuals.” The Ijlas gave a clear indication that the Ulama are gearing themselves more effectively than before in understanding the problems faced by society as well as the community and presenting their solutions.
