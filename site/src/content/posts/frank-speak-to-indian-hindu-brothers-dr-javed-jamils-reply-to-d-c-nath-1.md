**This is a point to point reply to Former IB Special Director, Mr D C Nath’s letter to me entitled, “Frank Speak to Indian Muslims”.**

![](https://javedjamilhome.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/09/58ca2-1btlxz9uf5t0aufdk56og6a.jpeg?w=736)  

L**etter from DC Nath**

October 6, 2015

Dear Dr. Jamil,

**Subject: Frank Speak to Indian Muslims**

‘Hope this finds you in the best of spirits and good health. Considering that you are an erudite scholar and would as such be an excellent representative of the Muslim community in India, we thought of putting across to you a few issues and questions, requesting for your response at your convenience.

**_Let us, however, first of all, put this straight. We are non-communal, nationalist and professedly apolitical. Also, we, with all the strength at our command, state that we are definitely not anti-Muslim by any stretch of imagination._**

My **Response**

_In the Name of God, Kind-Merciful, the Only One worthy of Worship, every other entity, animate or inanimate, great or holy, being His humble Servant_

**_Dear Mr. Nath_**

**_I highly appreciate your efforts to have a frank speak to Indian Muslims. I am sure you have framed this letter with the best of YOUR abilities and knowledge. My answers and comments to your remarks will appear in the red. Let me however make it clear at the very outset that my reply will be as frank as your letter, and while it may appear to be aggressive at times, it will ultimately be aimed at building a cohesive, prosperous and peaceful India. I am primarily a believer in the goodness of religion as such, and hate communal hatred, which I associate less with religion than with the desire to dominate. I believe if the followers of Hindu religion and Islam combine, they can defeat the onslaught of the so-called “modernism”, which is based on the negation of religion, godliness and religious morality and revolves around commercialisation of everything including human susceptibilities._**

**_Before starting to reply, I will like to assert that we Muslims do not only consider ourselves to be Indians by the core of our heart but also feel that we have the better claim to be true Indians than Hindu brothers. At the time of Partition, there were Hindus living in the current Indian lands for whom obviously, there was no other choice but to live in their native places, as they very well knew that the new India would have their domination much bigger than in the un-partitioned India. If there was no Partition, Muslims would have formed more than 30 per cent of the population, which is less than half in the current India. The Hindu Indians who migrated from Pakistani lands clearly showed that their love for their native land was much less than their desire to be part of a dispensation which would have domination of Hindus. So they preferred religion over their land. The same is true for those Muslims who migrated to Pakistan from Indian lands. But the current Indian Muslim population chose to stay in their native land despite having the choice of migrating to Pakistan. They ran the risk of being part of a system where they may have to face huge risks to their survival, and where their religious values might be under constant attack, and yet they chose to remain here because they loved their native land and were ready to make sacrifices for this._**

**_We Muslims are committed to make India a peaceful and prosperous country and cooperate with our fellow Hindu brothers and all other Indians belonging to any religion or no religion. But at the same time, we are ready to confront any individual or group that seeks to undermine India’s peace, harmony and collective prosperity. We will do it according to all the means available to us in the best interests of a united India. Of course, we will love to confront the obscurantists through peaceful means by way of intellectual dialogue, frank speak and social movements. And we are sure that the majority of Indians except a minority of hate mongers will ultimately support us in our effort._**

**_If we want to make India united, peaceful and prosperous, you will agree with me that it can only be achieved_**

1.  **_When all the communities and castes of the country are socially, economically and politically empowered;_**
2.  **_When Economic Disparity, General, Urban-Rural, Community to Community and Region to Region, in the country will be reduced to minimum;_**
3.  **_When all kinds of violence in the country, general crimes, communal violence, terrorism, violence against women, violence against week, are substantially reduced through an effective law mechanism. This requires a punishment equal to crime to every single criminal, irrespective of his identity or the identity of his victim._**
4.  **_When all the religions of the country unite in upholding religious morality in social and family issues rather than following the West-inspired Commercial Morality, and when we eradicate all forms of addictions, sex businesses, gambling, betting and immoral relationships._**

**_Coming back to your letter,_**

**_Many thanks for your appreciation of me. I am an individual, but I will try to represent my community in the best possible way. Where I have any opinion of myself as an individual, I will specifically mention that it is my personal opinion._**

**_As claimed by you, unfortunately, your present letter does not appear to conform to your “non-communal” position. It also does not appear to justify your claim that “that we are definitely not anti-Muslim by any stretch of imagination.” At least it shows that if you are not communal by heart, your inner feelings are very much overshadowed by the communal propaganda unleashed by certain forces for centuries. And unfortunately “communal” in India means “anti-Muslim”. The Hindutva lobby, the so-called protectors and promoters of Hindu religion, Hindu Culture, and Hindu Nationalism, has much less to do with the protection or defence of Hindu religion or country and much more to do with spreading hatred against Muslim community and keeping them backward, torturing them and keeping them in perpetual fear. This they believe will ensure their own hegemony in the country and they will be able to monopolise political, economic and social resources._**

_If Hindutva Brigade had been preaching religious values enshrined in Hindu Dharma, there was nothing bad about it. If they had been fighting for the rights of Hindus, even then it would not have created much of havoc._ **_The problem with them is that their definition of Hindutva, in practice if not in theory, is based on their severe aversion to Muslims and everything that is or can be made to look linked to them._** _They are less interested in introducing Hindu vision to the constitution and more in demolishing Muslim personal laws._ **_They are not too much fond of building temples; they are more concerned about demolishing mosques. They love singing Vande Mataram not because of its inherent beauty but because it irks Muslims who find hard to worship the land instead of the Creator of the land. Even if they do not read their scriptures with any regularity, they would want to impose it on Muslim students. They are not concerned about saving Hindu lives from fellow Hindus; they derive some sort of pleasure if Muslims are killed. Their love of cow, turning an animal into a holy entity, has grown over the centuries because this provides them ammunition to hit Muslims.\* They are not campaigning for bringing comforts to their fellow religionists; they are more interested in teasing and harassing Muslim populace._**

· **_In recent days Muslim leaders have made it clear again that they will support a total, nationwide and effective ban on cow slaughter. This is wholly to respect the sentiments of fellow countrymen, and they have never demanded ban on Pork, despite their huge aversion for it. (Why Hindus have not demanded ban on Alcohol and Sex outside marriage, which I am sure all religions including Hinduism condemn is hardly understandable.) Even Mughal kings had banned cow slaughter. One specific saying of Prophet Muhammad (Peace be upon him!) shows that he had a disliking for meat of cows though it was not prohibited. This was on account of certain diseases associated with cow meat. The diseases associated with Pigs were much more dangerous; so it was totally prohibited. Pork may cause Taeniasis (due to T. solium) and Trihinellasis both of which can cause sudden death or paralysis, and pig farming has caused big epidemics of Swine Flu killing tens of millions of people in last hundred years. Cow meat causes Taeniasis (due to T. Saginata), which is not normally fatal and cows have occasionally caused a syndrome of brain diseases (Mad Cow Syndrome) in Europe. To make this an issue of hatred for Muslims has no justification whatsoever, and whosoever slaughters cows in areas where it is banned must be tried under the law rather than making it a communal issue. Violation of all laws in India is common, not this law alone, due to the failure of Administration to effectively implement them. But in no case of violation of law, people can be allowed to take law into their own hands, and the culprit cannot be given a punishment more than what is prescribed in law._**

**_This has to be asserted again that if Muslims are supporting ban on cow slaughter, it is entirely in respect to the sentiments of Hindus despite the fact that in Islam there is no place of worship for any object other than God, and they can argue that there is no reason why humans should regard a specific animal (including its excreta) holy, and holier than other animals some of which are no less beneficial to humanity. Academically speaking, as a medical professional I find it hard to understand the difference between the urines of cow and buffalo, goat or camel. But irrespective of what the logic says, we agree that it is for Hindus to decide for themselves what is worth worshipping and what is holy for them. We respect this right, and would not oppose a ban on cow slaughter. At the same time, we will expect from our Hindu brothers that they should launch a big campaign to ban Alcohol, Drugs, Smoking and Commercial sex, which kill millions of people every year. And if they ban pork, they will be saving their own community members from diseases._**
