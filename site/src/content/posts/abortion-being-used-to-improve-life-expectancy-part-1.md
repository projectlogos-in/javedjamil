#### **Life Expectancy at Birth Vs True Life Expectancy**

[https://www.instagram.com/p/BO4R\_0cAFPh/](https://www.instagram.com/p/BO4R_0cAFPh/)

**Life** expectancy, as understood in the current medical sciences, is the expected average age of death in a specified population and is conventionally calculated from the time of birth1. It is usually believed that life expectancy has increased with the arrival of the modern medicine. The life expectancy before the beginning of the transition is estimated to have varied between 20 and 35. It is also agreed upon that once a person crossed his infant age, his chances of reaching the old age were high in all parts of the history; it still holds true. Scientists agree that the main reasons behind the steep rise of life expectancy have been sanitation, nutrition and medical knowledge1.

**Neither the definition of life expectancy nor the commonly described reasons behind the increase in life expectancy are beyond challenge. The definition, for example, includes consideration of infant mortality rate but excludes consideration of foetal mortality rate. The reasons include sanitation, nutrition and medical knowledge but excludes growing rate of induced abortions.**

There have been attempts by certain quarters to calculate life expectancy after the infant age, say. Obviously such a change in the definition would suit the countries and societies having high infant mortality rates. They would not then have to face the stigma caused by their positioning very low in the table of life expectancy.
