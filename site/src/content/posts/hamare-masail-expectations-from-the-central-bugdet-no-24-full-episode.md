#### Published on 24 Mar 2014

A general expectation is always there with the central budget, same expectation runs in Muslim community’s mind to get some extra advantage. What they can get from this Budget, is the topic for today’s discussion. To discuss this important issue, anchor Khureed Rabbani welcomes the experts like, Dr Javed Jamil (Economist), Dr. Zafar Mehmood (Chairman, Zakaat Foundation), Kamaal Faruqi (Secretory, Samajwadi Party) and Afsar-ul-Hasan (Journalist). To know the views, watch this show.

After the independence of India, the condition and participation of the minority community is a burning issue. This show catches the eyes of the viewer on the untouched and important issues related to the minority community, especially Muslims. After any important matter or incident, this show grabs that topic and relates & compares it with the Muslim community. The persons who relate to the topic provide their views and guidance. To know and connect with the issues of Muslim community, keep watching this talk show.
