#### **Life expectancy vis-à-vis abortion**

We will see below how abortion affects the life expectancy if its starting point changes from the birth to conception (True Life Expectancy). I have the country wise statistics of birth rate and life expectancy at birth, but despite a long research I could not find a complete country wise list of the ratio of abortion in all countries. I have however found the list of the abortion ratios (per 100 pregnancies) of all the political regions of the world and of some important countries. Despite this lacuna, there will be hardly any problem in understanding the moot point in discussion.

#### **4\. Life Expectancy in developed countries**

It is evident from the above that if foetuses are included in the human population, life expectancy (TLE) shows a sea change:

*   The life expectancy of Japan, which is ranked 1 in the table, goes down from 82.6 to 54.51 and its rank goes down from first to 48th.
*   The life expectancy of the US goes down from 78.2 to 50.90, a slide from 38th rank to 74th.
*   The life expectancy of the UK goes down from 79.4 to 47.70, a slide from 22nd rank to 97th.
*   The life expectancy of Australia goes down from 81.2 to 47.09, a slide from 5th rank to 102nd.
*   The life expectancy of Sweden goes down from 80.9 to 25.24, a slide from 7th rank to 156th.
*   The life expectancy of Russia goes down from 65.5 to 22.92, a slide from 137th rank to 159th.

The above again represents the few years old scenario. But the current position may only be marginally different. It can also be seen that while in the top 50 countries according to life expectancy at birth, the “developed” countries lead the table with more than two third majority; in the list of top 50 countries according to life expectancy at conception, they are less than one third.

#### **5\. Religious influence on life expectancy**

It is clear from the above that the degree of religious influence in a society affects life expectancy at conception in a remarkable way. Christianity and Islam both are clearly against abortions. This is also true of Judaism and other religions including Hinduism, Jainism and Buddhism. While the overall average TLE of countries having dominant Christian population is not good, the Christian countries in Western Europe like Switzerland and Belgium where the religious influence is strong have highest life expectancy. #### **6\. Life Expectancy in India**

True Life Expectancy of Indians must ideally have been among the highest, as India is a country with strong religious inclinations. Both Hindus and Muslims, which are the largest and second largest communities, have high degree of faith in God and morality. India is also emerging as an economic giant, and its medical infrastructure is capable of dealing with all kinds of problems. Life expectancy is still much better than the big five. It is 64.7 at birth and 53.05 at conception (TLE). However, it is not as high as it might have been, and apart from the poor management, low level of personal and social hygienic conditions causing life threatening infections, an important cause is the rising incidence of female foeticide. Due to the cultural influences among Hindus — dowry is still a big issue — where female foeticide is very high, the average life expectancy (TLE) of females is much lower than that of males. I have no exact statistics with me, but with overwhelming majority of abortions being those of females, this is the inevitable outcome.
