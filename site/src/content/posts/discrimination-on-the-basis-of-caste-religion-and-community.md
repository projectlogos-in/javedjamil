#### Frank Speak to Indian Hindu Brothers: Dr. Javed Jamil’s Reply to D C Nath — 9

**This is a point to point reply to Former IB Special Director, Mr D C Nath’s letter to me entitled, “Frank Speak to Indian Muslims”.**

![](https://javedjamilhome.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/09/22a79-0dffxpkfarveqie0g.jpg?w=736)  

**_In the Name of God, Kind-Merciful, the Only One worthy of Worship, every other entity, animate or inanimate, great or holy, being His humble servant_**

#### DC Nath

> 4) Will you be kind enough to go through the attached leaflet on “Think India”, based on facts and facts only?

> Do you have any disagreement with its contents?

#### Javed Jamil

I need not go and read that for the time. I think my answers will almost cover everything.

#### DC Nath

> **_1)_** **_Are you aware that the then Prime Minister Manmohan Singh had declared, in violation of his oath of office not to discriminate on the basis of caste, religion and community, that the minority community had the first right to the nation’s resources?_**

#### Javed Jamil

Manmohan Singh’s words had no constitutional implications and were only symbolic and metaphorical. Such symbolisation is common. Many have said in the past “Dalits have more rights than others”, ”Hindus and Hindu culture have more rights than others”, “Poor have more rights than others, etc”. Irrespective of what he said, what to speak of having the first right to Indian resources, Muslims, in effect, have the last right. What would otherwise explain their less than adequate representation in all political institutions, all private and public services, all industries and business establishments, and their lowest per capita income and lowest average per capita wealth. Hindutva lobby and you, instead of highlighting the hollowness of Man Mohan’s claim, have chosen to present his words as an evidence of favour to Muslims. How ridiculous!

#### DC Nath

> **_2)_** **_Did you have an opportunity to go through, rather, study the unique research- based book, “The Majority Report” by Shri R K Ohri and Shri J P Sharma? After a reading of Chapter IV of that book, as we told the Prime Minister, you will find it difficult to hold back tears rolling down your cheeks._**

#### Javed Jamil

If you really understand the conditions of Muslims with a compassionate heart, not only will your eyes shed more tears but you heart will also bleed profusely. But alas! You create a melodrama of your own sufferings, ignoring the truth that you (mainly Upper Caste Hindus) monopolise all the resources and institution of the country, and rejoice at the sufferings of fellow Indians who happen to have got enlightened through the Final version of God’s Religion based on absolute commitment to One Creator and Controller of the world and peace and justice for all.

#### DC Nath

> 3) May we also request you to go through the three circulatory mails?

> **_i)._** **_No Scope For Any Further Concessions Or Other Considerations For Muslim Brothers India._**

> **_ii)._** **_Minority Care — How Much More?_**

> **_iii)._** **_How Much More Will The Muslim Community Be Pampered? And, Why? Is It A Political Necessity For Bihar Elections Or For Survival? Serious Security Implication._**

#### Javed Jamil

I think this is not required anymore. We have already debated such nonsense umpteen times.

Bihar Elections have proved the exact opposite. In order to polarise the voters, Hindutva lobby played the communal and Upper caste card (RSS opposing Reservation) to the hilt. After Babri Masjid demolition and Gujarat riots, perhaps never has India’s reputation as a tolerant country suffered as much as in the last 40 days. And it is no coincidence that this has coincided with the Bihar elections. If NDA wins Bihar, it will signal doom for India; if it loses, lights will perhaps be seen at the end of the tunnel

#### DC Nath

> 4) Why are you against the introduction of the Common Civil Code? Does this go against any norm of Islamic theology?

#### Javed Jamil

I will start answering this question in next emails in a series of articles explaining why CCC is not acceptable. There are certain problems which have emerged owing to incomplete understanding of Islam and wrongful implementations; but these problems can and will be solved only within the parameters of Islam. The overwhelming majority of Muslims believe in Islamic laws, and this is their personal matter. Others can present their views but cannot enforce their own laws on us.

#### DC Nath

> 5) Would you support a policy of-reservations now in vogue? Or, you would agree to reservation only on economic criterion.

#### Javed Jamil

Our position is clear. For around 65 years, reservation policy is being followed in India. Till 1989, it was purely for Hindu Dalits, after Mandal Commission it was extended to include OBC Hindus with some Muslim castes also benefiting marginally in some of the states. As even Prime Minister has vowed to continue with the reservation policy, the Central government must come up with a clear plan to guarantee Muslims reservations, both in education and jobs, because they are even more backward than the Hindu Backward and are being denied reservation on the ground of their religion, which is a clear case of discrimination on the basis of religion. On the other hand, rich Hindus are being provided virtual reservation on the ground of their being rich in private institutions. If and when reservation on the ground of religion and caste (Hindu Dalits and Hindu OBCs) are withdrawn, Muslims will also give up their share in revolution. If reservation policy on the ground of economic deprivation is adopted, these private institutions awarding degrees to the rich despite their low merits should also be nationalised and run only on the merit basis. This alone will be true justice.

#### DC Nath

> 6) Is there any “scriptural” bar against Muslim women not allowed to take up any job? That restricts family’s total income with resultant consequences — leading to staying in “ghetos” and developing what is widely known as “gheto” mentality”. **_Incidentally, Lt Gen. (retd) Zameer Uddin Shah, the current Vice Chancellor, Aligarh Muslim University (AMU), supports this view. He says, “Muslims lag behind because they kept their women enslaved”._**

#### Javed Jamil

There is absolutely no “scriptural” bar on Muslim women. But there are certain parameters under which people, both men and women, have to work. This question will be discussed in detail later when we discuss the rights of women in Islam.

#### DC Nath

> 7) Are you aware that, resultantly, the members of the minority Muslim community develop “no go areas” for the police and others, especially in all metropolitan cities in the country?

#### Javed Jamil

If there are any “no go areas” in India, they are the areas controlled by Hindu Naxalites, Maoists and Ulfa activists. And of course the Hindutva Brigade is also a part of the “No Go Area”. This is why they have never been caught, prosecuted and punished despite their venomous attacks against minorities, despite their direct involvement in destruction of Babri Masjid against the court orders and despite their involvement in riots killing thousands of people belonging to minority communities. Ironically, the Hindutva Brigade presents 1984 anti-Sikh riots as a counter to the Gujarat riots forgetting the fact that in both cases it was communal Hindu mobs that killed minority members and that RSS and Hindutva lobbies were firmly behind Indira Gandhi and Rajiv Gandhi in those times and were on the forefront of instigating hatred against Sikh community. That was perhaps the only time when Hindutva Brigade was eulogising Gandhi family apart from support to Indira Gandhi in 1971 war.

#### DC Nath

> 8) Are you aware that the Muslim brothers in India are so well looked after here that an educated Muslim youth is not prepared to go out and settle down in any of the over 50 beautiful Muslim majority countries in the Middle East?

#### Javed Jamil

This is because Muslims love their country. Hindus go and settle in foreign lands, even in Muslim countries, because for them Lakshmi is more important than anything else. I can guarantee you that if countries like UAE, even Saudi Arab become more open to immigration policy, more Hindus will apply for citizenship than Muslims. Muslims are by nature less worldly, tolerant and patient people, always thanking God for whatever He gives to them. As I have already stated, Indian Muslims did not leave for Pakistan even at the time of Partition when the temptation was at its maximum.
