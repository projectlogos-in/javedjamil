#### A Systematic Study of the Holy Qur’an — PART II: PHILOSOPHY

![](https://javedjamilhome.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/09/93831-1bkbj8r-4kf5hpamhlaopyw.jpeg?w=736)  

### **Qur’an on Christianity and Judaism**

**As** has been clarified before in no uncertain terms, the Qur’an describes Muhammad as the last of more than one hundred thousand Ambassadors chosen by God to guide the mankind. They all were true envoys of God; and whatever they preached was in accordance with His commands. **The Book of Islam does not only recognise the founders of Christianity and Judaism but also glorifies them as among the greatest of men ever born**. But while Qur’an pays them the greatest of honour, it does not fail to criticise their so-called followers who have, for their own selfish ends, distorted their messages**. Qur’anic style of criticism however has the unique feature that it is based on facts some of which were not even known to the clergy of these religions**; furthermore, it **does not show any signs of bias or unwarranted malice towards them**. Some of them are even praised for their candidness and spiritual eminence. Qur’an is extremely severe in its criticism of Jews for failing to follow a large number of God’s Ambassadors, many of whom had to face harshest possible persecution at their hands; some of them were brutally murdered. It also takes Christian priests to task for converting Jesus, the Apostle, the Word and the Spirit of God into His Only Son, a concept that looks untenable at the very outset. But even in criticism, the Qur’an does not fail to condone those People of Book who have been honest in realizing the truth; and have proved the integrity of their character by their actions. God’s intention is not to unnecessarily humiliate them; He prefers to remind them that the founders of their religion were all His Ambassadors; and if they are His real devotees, they must waste no time in recognising His Last Messenger, Muhammad. PBUH

Christians must realise the fact that out of all those who do not believe in the fundamentals of Christianity **_it is only Muslims who love all those whom Christians love:_** God, Mary, Jesus, John the Baptist, Holy Spirit and all other messengers of God including Abraham, Ishmael, Isaac, Jacob, Joseph, Moses, Job, Jonas, David and Solomon (Peace be upon them!). Love for them is an inseparable part of Islamic Faith. Not only their belief in Jesus and respect for him and his mother are profound, majority of them also believe that **_Jesus will come again in the world and will unite Muslims and Christians against the Evil forces_** _ruling the world._ And most Muslim scholars believe that the time of Second Advent of Christ is not far away.

**_Qur’an glorifies Jesus_ (Peace be upon him!) _and Mary (_Peace be upon her!)) _in no uncertain terms._ Muslims regard Jesus as the penultimate Prophet of Islam (the last being Muhammad (Peace be upon him!)) and “Word”, “Spirit” and “Ambassador of God_”. Further, it might sound incredible to Christians but the fact is that apart from several stories common in Qur’an and Bible, there are many events in the lives of Jesus and Mary that are described only in Qur’an;_ Bible is silent about them. For example, Qur’an describes in detail the story of how Virgin Mary went on to take bath and The Holy Spirit announced to her the birth of Jesus as a _Wonder_ of God. It also describes events related to the birth of Jesus, difficulties faced by Mary, the help by God, slandering of Mary by the community and Jesus’ miraculous speaking in the cradle to silence his mother’s detractors. Not only this, Qur’an also describes in detail the birth of Mary herself and her miraculous childhood. It also describes the birth of John the Baptist. These all stories are not present in any of the versions of Gospel. At the same time, Qur’an does also not fail to rectify certain misconceptions about Jesus’ life.**

Christians and Jews should also know that Quran coins for them a very respectable term, _“_**_Ahl-e-Kitab”,_** the People of the Testament because they have been reading earlier parts (Torah, Psalm sand Gospel) of the Testament of God. To affirm their proximity to Muslims, several practical measures have been taken. The foods of Muslims and People of Book have been declared lawful for both. (5:5). Taking another major leap towards bonding Muslims with the People of Testament, **Muslims have been permitted to marry Christian and Jew women; they are not required to change their religion**. **_(5: 5)_** Muslim rulers have also taken care to judge the internal affairs of Christians (as well as of Jews) on the basis of their own laws.

As said above, Qur’an pays great respect to all those who are revered by Christianity. But while Christian priests are admired for certain qualities, Qur’an does not fail to put the matter **straight by criticising Christianity in _matters that dilute_** _or corrupt the absolute monotheism_**.** Jesus was a man of truth like all other Ambassadors of God but he was a chosen _human being_, who would guide mankind in a way instructed by God. God is Absolute, Infinite and Perfect. Not even for an iota of time can He, in part or as a whole, become limited or bound by space or time. For whatever aim, God can never be caught by human beings and killed. Jesus was very special; whatever he did and said was in accordance with what God wanted him to say or do. This is another matter that many of his sayings or doings were not correctly recorded or interpreted. He was the SON of GOD, not biologically but metaphorically, because God the Absolute _creates and not reproduces_. Reproduction is a sign of dependence. It can be proven from Bible that, at several places, Jesus called God as Father of not only himself but also of all humans; he called himself “Son of Man” at several places. Humans were called “sons of God” at several places in both Old and New Testaments. God may be like father in the sense that He is Kind towards His creatures in the same way as a father is expected to be kind to his children. But to call Him Father in the Biological sense would deprive Him of His Absoluteness. God is one that encompasses the whole universe (even any other universes that might be existing) and controls it. One who can create anything need not reproduce. Furthermore, Son of God cannot be a role model for sons of men. Jesus was born without a father as a special miracle of God like Adam was born without parents.

Islam (literally meaning PEACE, and defined as TOTAL SUBMISSION TO GOD) is not a new System in the sense that its foundations are the same. It is the same System, which Adam, Abraham, Moses and Jesus (Peace be upon them!) followed, except that God _remodelled the constitution_ to suit new requirements. The basic philosophy of Belief in One God, Belief in His Ambassadors and Testaments, Belief in Hereafter and Belief in GOOD ACTIONS remains the same. But as Qur’an is for all times to come, it sets guidelines concerning all matters of life. Unlike Christianity that primarily seeks to reform an individual, Islam also gives well-defined principles about family and social life, including social, political, legal and economic systems.

> **In the name of God, the Kind, the Merciful**

### **CHRISTIANITY AND JUDAISM IN THE QUR’AN**

#### **Followers of previous scriptures welcome to Islam**

Certainly for all among those who have committed now, and those who in the past believed in Judaism or were Christians or Sabians and believed in God and the Last Day, and conducted righteously, there is **a rich dividend in store with their Lord; they have nothing to worry or despair about.** (2: 62/A)

(Another translation (word to word) Certainly for all those who committed and became rightly guided, and became helpers and upright and got fully convinced about God and Hereafter, there is Dividend (in store) with God; they have nothing to worry or despair about.)

Of course, there are the people who have committed themselves (to Islam), Jews, Sabians, Christians, Magians (or Zoroastrians) and pantheists; God will surely give His verdict on the Day of Halt (on the disputes) between them; of course God alone is the witness of everything. (22: 17)

#### Jesus not biological son

They claim that God fathered a son: Force is His. On the contrary His is all that is in the Cosmic Tiers and the Earth: Everything obeys Him. He is the innovator of the Cosmic Tiers and the Earth: When He decides to give an order, He just says, “Go” and it goes. (2: 116–117)

They claim that God has fathered a son. Force is His! He is Wealthy-beyond-Need; whatever is in the Cosmic tiers and the Earth is His; and certainly you have no authority to claim this. Do you speak regarding God what you do not know? (10: 68–69)

How could He have a (biological) son when He has never had a spouse; and He created everything and He knows everything. (6: 101)

#### Mary a woman of truth

Christ the son of Mary was only an Ambassador; a number of Ambassadors passed away before him. His mother was an honest woman. Both of them used to take food (as every human being). Look how We clarify our arguments to them; and even after that, look how they remain in delusion! (5: 75)

#### Infant Jesus speaks

Narrate in the Book how Mary secluded herself away from her family members to a place in the eastern side. Then she put a curtain between her and them. At this juncture, We sent Our Spirit-angel to her; then he made appearance before her in a perfectly human form. She said: “I seek the shelter of Kind-Lord from you if you harbour any awe of Him,” He replied: “I am only an envoy of your Lord (sent) for announcing the gift of a holy obedient-boy.” She asked; “How can I have the obedient-boy when no man has ever been in my contact and I have not been characterless.” He replied: “Even despite that it will happen, and your Lord says that ‘it is easy for Me; and We will make him a (big) clue and a favour from Our side to mankind’; and it is an order that is being enforced.” Then she conceived, and she shifted with (the baby) to a remote place. (19: 16–22)

Subsequently, she brought him to her people carrying him (in her lap). They said: “Mary! You have certainly taken an unprecedented step. Sister of Aaron! Your father was not an evil man nor was your mother corrupt.” Following that she signalled towards him. (At this) they said: “How would we talk to one who is merely a kid in the cradle.” (The child Jesus) spoke: “I am indeed a subject of God. He has endowed me with the Book and has made me a prophet. Further, He has made me auspicious wherever I am (supposed to be), and has given me instructions regarding Prayer (of blessedness) and Purifying Charity as long as I am alive. In addition, He has made me affectionately fond of my mother, and has not made me a hardened despot. And I have been bestowed upon peace the day I was born, the day I die and the day I am born again.” This is all about Jesus, the statement of truth, which they are confused about. (19: 27–35)

Christ the Apostle and Statement (Kalimah) of God

People of the Book! Make no exaggerations in your system (of beliefs and laws); and do not make any statement about God beyond the mandate. Jesus Christ, the son of Mary, was of course an Ambassador of God, His Declaration, and a Spirit from Him, which He brought to Mary. Hence, commit yourselves to God and His Ambassadors. Further, do not claim Trinity. Stop this! It will be better for you. Surely God is the only god. Subhan (the Engine) being His, it is (below His status) to have a son. His is whatever is in the Cosmic tiers and the Earth. And God is enough as counsellor. (4: 171)

#### Christ worshipper of God

Christ does never hate being a subject in service to God, nor do His closest Executive-angels. However, there are people who hate to worship Him and are arrogantly proud; Subsequently God will assemble them all together before Him (for judgment). (4:172)

They have acted in dissent who claimed that “Christ the son of Mary is in fact God.” And (in contrast) Christ said: “Children of Israel! Worship God, my Lord as well as your Lord.” Of course, if anyone worships extra-gods, God will bar him from the Paradise, and his Fire will be his place of shelter. Subsequently, there will no helpers for the brutes. (5: 72–73)

#### **Baptism of God**

God’s is the true Hue: And who is better in imparting the hue than God? It is He we worship. (2: 138)

#### **Christ raised**

Moreover, their statement was that “We killed Christ, Jesus the son of Mary, the Ambassador of God.” And (the truth is that) they could not kill or crucify him; instead it was only an illusion for them. Certainly the people who argue about it are in a state of doubtfulness about (what happened); they know nothing about it except indulging in speculation; and what is absolutely sure is that they did not kill him. On the contrary, God lifted him up towards Him. God is Powerful and Wise. And everyone from among the People of the Book without exception should better get convinced before his death about (Jesus); and when the Day of Halt comes, he will act as witness against them. (4: 157–159)

Jews called Uzair “son of God” and the Christians called Christ “Son of God”. They are merely statements, (rumours spread) by their mouths; they are just imitating the statement of the dissenters of previous times. (9: 30)

#### Christians nearest to Muslims

You will find Jews and pantheists most hostile to the people who have committed themselves (to Islam); and closest in relationship with the people who have committed are the people who call themselves Christians; this is because among them there are learned persons and ascetics and they do not indulge in arrogance. Further, when they hear what has been dispatched to the Ambassador, you will see their eyes full of tears, as they recognise the mandate, uttering “Our Lord! We commit ourselves; note our (names) along with the people who testify (the truth). We have no reason not to commit ourselves to God, and what reached us as part of the Mandate; and we desire that our Lord will include us in the community of good people.” (5: 82–84)

#### Religion of Abraham

They say: “Become Jew or Christian in order to become rightly guided.” You tell them: “Instead (I would join) the Monotheistic legacy of Abraham; and he was not one of the pantheists.” State: “We believe in God, in what has been communicated to us, and in what was communicated to Abraham, Isma’il, Isaac, Jacob, and the Tribes, in what was delivered to Moses and Jesus, and in everything that was delivered to the prophets from their Lord: We make no discrimination with anyone of them: And We are Seekers of Peace (Muslims) for His Pleasure. So if they accept as you accept, they have become rightly guided; but if they turn away, it is as if they (have fallen) in a trench; So God will make you sufficiently capable against them, and He is Hearer-Prescient. (2: 135–137)

“Or do you claim that Abraham, Isma’il Isaac, Jacob and the Tribes were in fact Jews or Christians?” State: Do they know better than God? Who is a bigger offender than those who conceal the Divine Evidence that they possess? God is not negligent of what you do! (2: 140)

#### Curse on Rejecters

Consequent upon their unjustness, We declared certain items prohibited for them that were healthy and were previously permitted to them — their being an obstacle in the way of the majority of the people seeking God’s Path, their exacting usury despite its being prohibited and their unjustifiably consuming the wealth of people. We have a painful punishment ready for the dissenters among them. (4: 160–161)

The people who dissented from among the Children of Israel were cursed upon by the tongue of David as well as Jesus the Son of Mary; this was because they disobeyed and committed excesses. They did not dissuade one another from indulging in the forbidden practices; what they used to do was indeed bad. (5: 78–79)

#### Rabbis must act right

Why do the rabbis and legal experts not dissuade them from indulging in sinful talks and from consuming what are unlawful? What they keep doing industriously is truly bad. Jews said, “God’s hand is tied.” Be their hands tied, and be they cursed for what they say! On the contrary, both His Hands are freely generous; He spends as He determines. Moreover, what is dispatched to you from your Lord accentuates aggression and dissent in most of them. Subsequently, We have imbued in them mutual animosity and hatred till the Day of Halt. Whenever they have ignited the fire of confrontation (against you), We have extinguished it. They keep trying to foment turmoil on the Earth; and God does not love the people fomenting turmoil. (5: 63–64)

We had struck a covenant with Children of Israel. Subsequently We dispatched ambassadors to them (for their guidance); whenever an ambassador would come with something that did not appeal to their inner instincts; they would either reject him as a false (prophet) or kill him. (5: 70)

#### Paradise for believers

Further, had the people of the Book committed themselves (to Qur’an), and been conscientiously dutiful, We would have nullified the (charges of) misdeeds from them, and had given them entry in the Gardens of Bliss. Had they put into practice Torah, Gospel and what has (now) been dispatched to them by their Lord, they would have enjoyed the (Nature’s provisions) from (the sky) above them and the earth beneath their feet. A group among them is on the right course; but the majority of them indulge in misdeeds. (5: 65–66)

The People of the Book (challengingly) ask you to bring down to them a book from the heaven. They had made even a bigger demand to Moses when they had said, “Show us God in person in full public view”; consequently they were struck with thunderstorm due to their unjustness. Following that they held cow as an object of worship despite the fact that clear evidences had come to them. Even then we granted them amnesty, and gave Moses a well-recognised (ruling) power. Further, We imposed Covenant of Mount (Sinai) over them. Subsequently, We asked them to enter the gate bowing humbly (to God) and told them never to break the rules of Sabbath; and We struck with them a substantial covenant. Then what followed was their breach of the Covenant, show of dissent towards the verses of God, murders of the Ambassadors in violation of the Mandate and their arrogant statement ,”Our hearts are the full-proof envelopes (not allowing any leaks).” On the contrary, God blocked their (hearts) because of their dissent; now (in continuation with their past behaviour) they, barring a few, do not commit themselves (to Qur’an). (4: 153–155)

#### Invitation to People of the Book

State: “People of the Book, do not make exaggerations in your System beyond the given Mandate; do not imitate the flimsy imaginations of previous generations who misguided many and remained unguided themselves away from the proper path. (5: 78)

State: “O People of the Book! Let us come to (the terms of a) proper declaration between you and us: that we will not worship anyone except God, we will not commit to extra-gods and we will also not hold lordly figures among ourselves in esteem in violation of God!” Following that if they turn back, tell them: “Remain witness (to the fact) that we have always been true Muslims, (finding peace in One God).” (3:64)

Moreover, there are persons among the People of the Book who, even if you entrust a big mould of currency to them, will honestly repay it; and there are others who, if you entrust them even a little currency, they will not pay it back unless you constantly keep demanding from them. This is because they claim that “there is no responsibility on us regarding the illiterates.” And they knowingly attribute false (rules) to God. (3: 75)

They are not a monolithic community. There is (also) a group among the People of the Book that keeps standing in the nights reciting the verses of God and prostrating (in prayers). They are committed to God and Hereafter, promote what is appreciable and campaign against what is unacceptable, and are prompt in actions of welfare. It is they who are the excellent performers. (3: 113–114)

And certainly there are persons among the people of the Book who commit themselves to God, to what has been dispatched to you and to what had been dispatched to them in all humility for the sake of God. They will not sell the verses of God in return of a small price. For them, there is a reward in store with God; and God is prompt in the Payment. (3: 199)

#### **Social Relations with People of the Book promoted**

Having meals (on invitation) by the Recipients of the Book is permitted to you and their having meals (on your invitation) is permissible for them; (5: 5)

Further, the women of character from among the women of the Commitment as well as from among the recipients of the Book are permitted for you (for marriage). (5: 5)
