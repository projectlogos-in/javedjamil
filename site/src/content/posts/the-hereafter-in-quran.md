#### A Systematic Study of the Holy Qur’an

![](https://javedjamilhome.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/09/47561-1w_v5a2glpccznw6fb4ng2q.jpeg?w=736)  

When we throw a thoughtful glance at the conditions prevailing in the present world, we find exploitation, persecution, oppression and, consequent upon them, suffering everywhere. The stronger is depriving the weaker of the same amenities of life which he thinks essential for himself. Innocents are being killed every day in big numbers. This pervading injustice is obviously of man’s own making; had mankind treaded the path of God, none could have impinged the rights of others. So if it is assumed that this is the only world and there is no other world to follow this world, it will lead to the stark conclusion that God is unjust (God forbid!) because **there is no discernible relief to the victims — a murdered person cannot get any relief, as he cannot regain life — — or to those who make huge sacrifices for the cause of humanity; and there is also no punishment for the perpetrators of these suffering**. And if God is unjust, he is just no God. The Absolute One has to be the absolutely Just unlike all other judges who often make mistakes. God will prove His justice by undoing this injustice in a way **that will satisfy all: the right and the wrong.** To achieve this ultimate aim, He made provision for another life after this life. He has given man a certain period of time to prove himself and to labour in accordance with the commands of God; if he acts according to his own whims, he is liable to punishment. The greatest punishment will of course be for the **repudiators of the Greatest Reality** of the Universe and His messengers; the**_absolute_** **repudiators** will abide forever in the hell. The greatest reward will be for those who have **recognised this** **Greatest Reality** and striven hard to do good deeds to earn His Pleasure; Paradise will be their eternal abode.

A question is often put forward: why will those born in non-Muslim families who have not had an opportunity to understand and follow Islam be punished with Eternal Fire? Does it not tantamount to injustice, they ask. To understand, we have to find analogies, for we have no other way to understand things accept by drawing parallels to the phenomena that are occurring before our eyes. It is the duty of each and every individual to know the law of the land; **one who transgresses these laws cannot simply be condoned on account of one’s ignorance**. In the same way, it is the duty of every mentally sound individual to recognise one’s creator and follow His bounds. If one fails in one’s duty, one cannot avoid punishment. Now, because, the Eternal punishment is for the “**repudiators**” (absolute) of Faith, it is left entirely for the judgement of God to decide whether or not a person truly repudiated the faith, **despite sufficient opportunities**; and His judgement will surely be such **as will satisfy everyone**, even the one condemned to Hell. Those who die immature and those who are mentally retarded will of course be exempt from the Grievous Punishment. Even Kufr (Repudiation) can be relative, and Allah’s punishment will depend upon the degree of Kufr of each individual. One should not conclude from this that believers will not be punished at all; for **the criteria of true faith is not just the declaration of the faith by one’s tongue but its manifestation in real life**.

What will happen to those, who have not joined Islam but have dedicated all their life to the welfare of fellow human beings, is a matter of conjecture. God cannot be unjust to them. Qur’an categorically declares that anyone who performs even an iota of good will receive its return, and anyone who commits even an iota of wrong, will likewise receive its return. When judging, God will of course take every action of the individual into account. **The judgement in their case will depend both on their Disbelief and their good deeds.**

**It can be noted that out of all the religious scriptures, Qur’an alone describes in great details the happenings related to Hereafter — the events leading to Doomsday, the Doomsday itself and the Life after Resurrection. The Book gives vivid accounts of both Paradise and Hell. Paradise will be a place where there will be permanent happiness and no worries or pains of any kind. Unlike in this world, the pleasures in Hereafter will be without any ill effects. The Hell on the other hand will have only pain and deprivation. Death will not be there — neither for the inhabitants of Paradise nor of the Hell. More and more people will keep on entering Paradise after undergoing their punishments in Hell; and only those will abide in the Hell forever whose repudiation in the judgment of God was absolute beyond any Pardon. The last word will of course be God’s.**

> In the name of God, the Kind, the Merciful

### **The Hereafter in The Holy Qur’an**

#### **Death and Hereafter approaching fast**

The Day is to come when We will call all the people along with their Leaders (Imams). (17: 71)

They say, “What! After we have become bones and dust, will we be resurrected as a new creation? ”State, “Even if you become stones or iron or the hardest matter in the creation that you can think of in your hearts.” Now they will ask you, “Who will cause us to return?” State, “The One Who formed you for the first time.” Now they will wag their heads towards you and ask, “When will his happen?” State, “May be it will happen sooner than later. That Day He will call you and you will respond along with His Acknowledgement, and you will think that you had a very brief stay.” (17: 49–52)

The (time of the declaration of the) Result of the mankind has come close, and still the people are obliviously sceptic. (21: 1)

Further, the people who dissented said, “Should we tell you about a man, who prophesies that when you are all disintegrated into pieces you will be in a new creation? Has he (simply) forged a false statement against God or is there a Jinn with him?” The (truth) on the contrary is that it is the people not convinced about the Next World who are in far-fetched darkness. (34: 7–8)

#### Resurrection easy for God

Does the man calculate that We cannot assemble his bones? The truth on the contrary is that We are capable of setting even the marks of his fingertips. (75: 3–4)

Mankind! Be reverently dutiful to your Lord and feel the awe of the Day when no father can face the result in place of his son nor a son can in place of his father. Undoubtedly the promise of your Lord is a Mandated Truth. Let the life of this world not swell your ego; and let not the Ego make you arrogant about God. (31: 33)

Further, as the Statement (of truth) will prove itself to them, We will bring out an (amazing) living being walking on the Earth to (confront) them. This (creature) will converse with them arguing that the mankind failed to make itself convinced about the (truthfulness) of Our Arguments. Further, the day will come when we will assemble groups of those who negated Our Arguments, from among all the generations, and they will be lined till they all come and God asks: “Did you negate My Arguments despite not having any command on the knowledge of (the reality) and what has been your performance?” (27: 82–84)

#### All endeavours to be considered

Now the endeavours of anyone who performs virtuous deeds and is committed (to Islam) will not be dismissed; We keep recording them for him. Further, , there is a ban on a locality which We destroyed that it would not make a comeback till Gog and Magog are set loose and they move victoriously from every side. And with that the mandated promise will come closer. Then arrives the time when the eyes of every dissenter will watch (the events) with fixed stare (uttering), “Ah! Damn with us! We have been oblivious of the (truth); and on the contrary we have truly been clear offenders!” (21: 94–97)

#### **Events before and on Terrible Day**

O Mankind! Feel the Awe of your Lord! Undoubtedly the Quake of the Moment will be a massive thing. The day you witness it mothers will become forgetful of breastfeeding their babies and pregnant women will abort their burdens, and you will find the mankind in a state of intoxication even if they were not drunk; but the punishment of God will be severe. (22: 1–2)

Now if you dissent how will you be alert about the day that will make boys look like girls/old? The sky will be broken. His promise will be enacted. (73: 17–18)

Somebody questions, “When is the Day of Halt (going to happen)?” So, (it will unfold) once the power of vision becomes lightening fast, the moon disappears (or eclipses) and the sun and moon are joined together.. (75: 6–9)

By the ones that destroy with violence, by the ones that act smartly, by the ones that move speedily! Now they move, one ahead of the other; now they execute the command. On that day something will be shaken; and there will follow the second blast. On that day hearts will be throbbing; and the eyes will be scary. They will discuss, “Will we be pushed back to our former state now? What! Will we become destroyed bones? They will say that it would be a return with huge loss. Now just this single blast will bring them on the surface (of the earth). (79:1–14)

Moreover, there is a ban on a locality which We destroyed that it would not make a comeback till Gog and Magog are set loose and they move victoriously from every side. (21: 95–96)

And there will come a time when (the truth of the statement) will unfold in front of them, when We will bring out a creature from the earth which will speak to them telling that the mankind has certainly not been true in conviction to Our guidelines. (27:82)

Now, wait for the day when the sky will bring a clearly visible smoke enveloping the people; it will be a grievous torment. (44: 10–11)

There will come a time when the Earth will be jolted with a massive Earthquake; and the Earth will bring out its burdens; and the mankind will wonder, “what has happened to it?” On that day she will describe her history in a way as communicated to her by her Lord. On that Day the people will proceed in groups to witness the (consequences of) their actions. (99:1–6)

The Calamity! What Calamity! And what will make you understand what the Calamity is? On that day the mankind will become like a spread out mattress (moths) and the mountains will become like fluffy wool. (101–105)

_(Note: Normally, many of these verses are taken to mean the events unfolding when Qayaamah sets in motion. But many of these verses may in fact indicate the turn of events before the actual Event. For example, lack of interest in breastfeeding, widespread abortions and turning of boys into girlish looks or change of sex are all the signs of the coming closer of Qayamah. Similarly I have a feeling that the disappearance of moon from its current position and falling on the Sun or its beginning to revolve round the Sun instead of the Earth may be some of the events taking place before the Doomsday. )_

When the sun (with its spacious light) is folded up; when the stars fall, losing their lustre; when the mountains vanish (like a mirage); when the she-camels, ten months with young, are left untended; when the wild beasts are herded together (in the human habitations); when the oceans boil over with a swell; when the souls are sorted out, (being joined, like with like); when the female (infant), buried alive, is questioned — for what crime she was killed; when the scrolls are laid open; when the world on High is unveiled; when the Blazing Fire is ignited to fierce heat; and when the Garden is brought near;- (then) shall each soul know what it has put forward. (81: 1–14)

The Day (will come) when We will roll the sky the way the register of the book is rolled; the way We produced the creation for the first time We will repeat it. This is a responsibility on Us; We will surely accomplish it. (21: 104)

Now, when the stars become suppressed, when the sky becomes spaced and when the mountains are blown and when the ambassadors are appointed a time — for what day are these (events) postponed? — For the Day of Selection. (77: 8–13)

When the sky is incised and becomes like a red rose.. (55: 37)

They tend to visualise the Day as too far; and We see it too close. On that Day the sky will become like molten brass, and the mountains will become like wool; and one friend will not speak to another friend despite their seeing each other. In order to avoid the punishment, the convict would even be willing to sacrifice his children, his wife and his brother and his kindred who gave him shelter (in the world), in fact all that is on the Earth; so that it could release him. (70: 6–14)

When the sky is split, and it hears (the command from) its Lord, and it is duty bound for that; and when the Earth is turned flat and brings out what is inside it becoming empty, and hears the (command of) God as it is duty bound to do that, (the message will be:) “O Mankind! Till now you were labouring hard towards God; now you will meet Him.” (84: 1–6)

The Mount is witness, the Book scripted in a scroll is witness; the House of Continuous Pilgrimage is witness; the High Roof is witness and the Swelling Ocean is witness, the Punishment from your Lord will indeed be a factual happening. None can avert it; on the Day when the Sky will be in a dreadful state of affairs and the mountains will be moving here and there. (52: 1–11)

When the Sky is broken; when the planets are spread; when the oceans are blown; and when the graves are scattered, everyone will know what he has sent forward and (what he has) left behind. . (82: 1–5)

And the Day comes when the Trumpet will be blown; now the creatures of the Cosmic spheres as well as the creatures of the Earth will be in a state of panic, with the exception of those whom God determines (as exempt), and all of them will present in disgraceful conditions. Furthermore, you now view the mountains considering them as immovable structures but (that Day) they will move the way the clouds move. The factory of God discharges all its (functions) with perfection. Undoubtedly He has information about whatever you do. (27: 87–88)

When the Event comes into happening, none will be in a position to deny it, the (event) that will elevate as well as humble; when the Earth will be thoroughly shaken and the mountains will be finely crumbled so that they will now become dust scattered far and wide; you will become three groups. Now there will be the Right Group of Colleagues — what will be the Right Group? And there will be the Left Group of Colleagues — What will be the Left Group? And the Toppers will of course be the Toppers. They will be the People of Close Circle in the Special Garden of Bliss. (56: 4–11)

Furthermore they did not hold God in the unparalleled esteem that He deserved. On the Day of Halt, the whole Earth will be in His little grasp and the Cosmic spheres will be rolled with His Right Hand. The Ultimate Force is His, and He is above all which you include (in worship) along with Him. As soon as the Trumpet is blown, all the (creatures) of the Earth and Cosmic spheres will have a stunning effect except the ones God determines (to exclude), Following that the Trumpet will be blown a second time and all of them will stand up with their sights restored. The Earth will brighten up with the Light of its Lord; the Book (of Records) will be put into effect, the prophets and standard-bearers will be asked to proceed and the judgement will be made with mandate; and no one will be wronged. The returns will be transferred to everyone, in accordance with what they performed; and God knows all that they do. (39: 67–70)

On the Day the Spirit and the Angels will stand in lines. None will speak except if signalled by God, the Kind Lord, and He will say what is suitable (for the occasion). (78: 38)

On that Day the Earth will be changed to a different Earth, and the Cosmic spheres too (to different kinds of Cosmic spheres); they will be produced before God, the One — (Overpowering Lord). Subsequently, you will see the sinners bound together in fetters, wearing garments of liquid pitch and their faces turning fiery. 14: 48–50)

On that day they will (have no option but to) follow the Caller; they simply cannot ditch him; all the voices will humble before the Kind Lord; hence, they will hear nothing but the sounds of their own steps. On that day no recommendation will benefit anybody except of the one signalled by God, one whose word is pleasant to Him. (20: 108–109)
