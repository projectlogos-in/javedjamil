**_In the Name of God, Kind-Merciful, the Only One worthy of Worship, every other entity, animate or inanimate, great or holy, being His humble servant_**

#### **_To continue_**

#### DC Nath

> · that the Islamic invaders have destroyed and razed to the ground a number of renowned Hindu temples, yet India had never retaliated by destroying any mosques mentioned or even a madrassa. We have not about the incident of Babri Mosque, which had been a clear case of a mosque having been built on existing Hindu Temple of Ramlala.

![](https://javedjamilhome.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/09/ac773-0gmuuic-5bcverlfl.jpg?w=736)  

#### Javed Jamil

Again, the historians will have a lot of argument on it. I do not call them “Islamic invaders”. They were just Muslim imperialists like Hindu and Christian imperialists of the history. There are only a few examples of destruction of places of worship whereas examples of huge grants to temples abound. In Punjab, Haryana and Rajasthan thousands of mosques have been turned into stables; many have been forcefully taken by the Government on the ground of preservation of historical monuments, and Muslims are not allowed to worship in them. The case of the destruction of Babri Masjid cannot be brushed aside on whimsical grounds given by you. If it had been a “clear case”, the forces of destruction would have waited for the “case” to be proven in the court. That they did not wait was in fact a clear proof that their “case” was not strong enough. The destruction violated every rule of the Constitution, every principle of social and moral propriety. The mosque was destroyed despite the fact that the case was pending in the court, and Muslims had categorically stated that they would abide by the decision of the court. It was a sheer display of mob power with no regard for the religious sentiments of another community and continues to be the biggest blot on the face of Secular and Democratic India. They forgot that the God worshipped in Babri Masjid was one God who is also the God of Hindus and was also the God of Sri Ram for whom I have huge respect in my heart. I have read Valmiki Ramayana in detail, and it is clear that Ram was an obedient devotee of God and a figure worthy of great respect. If Ram was alive, he would never have tolerated the destruction of a place of worship of God, the Lord of the universe, for building a temple for the worship of his idol. The destruction was the most glaring example of the emerging psyche of Hindutva Brigade that instigated taking law in their own hands, giving and implementing the judgment the way they like. Do Hindutva protagonists not have faith in Indian Constitution, despite the fact that not only the majority of the makers of the Constitution were Hindus but is also applied and interpreted mostly by Hindu officers and judges? The acts of insult to places of worship of Muslims and Christians including throwing of a specific meat in mosques, campaigns against banning Adhan are of day to day occurrence.

#### DC Nath

> · that such is the catholicity of the Hindus, they accept any one into their fold without any fuss. Hinduism does not have any policy of conversion, a tool indiscriminately used by the two Abrahamic religions, namely, Christianity and Islam.

#### Javed Jamil

This is nothing special with this so-called catholicity of Hindus. The whole Arab world was inhabited by pagans or fire worshippers. Instead of continuing the deviant practice adopted by their forefathers, they all converted to Islam. So were Persia, Indonesia and Malaysia and a large number of other lands in Central Asia, Europe and Africa. It has been a historical truth that whenever the new Messenger of God arrived on the earth with an unadulterated and updated version of God’s religion, it started attracting people due to its inherent appeal. Of course, initially they faced opposition and persecution at the hands of the leaders of mischief. When Islam came to India, people started accepting it as an escape from the worship of hundreds of thousands of deities in favour of absolute monotheism and as an escape from the brutal caste system prevailing here in favour of social equality and more advanced system of Permissions and Prohibitions. All the living Muslims in India know that their forefathers were Hindus but they have stuck to their religion even centuries after the demise of Mughal rule. If about one third of the population of Indian subcontinent converted to Islam, and continues to hold it so dear, it shows how convinced they are about their religion. Moreover, Islam literally meaning peace and defined as total submission to One God, is only a culmination of God’s earlier versions, which included Indian religions in their original (and not the present distorted) shape. In fact, Hindus showed greater obstinacy in remaining committed to their traditional beliefs, as two third of them did not get enlightened to the new vision. In contrast, Arabia, Persian, Malaysian, Central Asian and Indonesian population showed much bigger understanding of Islam, which did not condemn the old religions and scriptures but confirmed their truthfulness in their original form.

They have also tried to convert Muslims and Christians in the name of “Ghar Waapsi”, and as they claim their religion to be the oldest, they can easily use this ploy to convert. But the truth is that the rate of conversion depends upon the appeal of specific ideologies, short as well as long term. For example, communism attracted large number of people on account of its social principle of equal distribution and care for the labour, but it soon lost its appeal on account of the lack of spirituality and moral and family values.
