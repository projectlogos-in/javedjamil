Dear Sir,

You are very right when you state that to reduce the gap between rich and poor, the Corporations must be examined in details for their activities. I am attaching the same herewith for your reference. Kindly go through and let me have your ‘learned’ comments on the same.

In fact recently I have come across articles from USA and other Western countries which state that because of the Capitalism that they use for improving their economies have brought these countries to the precipice of destruction by having their national debts exceeding the GDP because of behaviour of the Corporates. Op Ed News had said that the Corporations established to provide food items to citizens have become so arrogant for earning money that the food supply has become ‘daily poison supply’ to the people.

Only recently I have read an article “EU’s demise, Trump presidency can boost India’s Economy” published in Sunday Guardian, sent on internet by Shri Come Carpentier that aske India to stop Cashless Societies, since it is a signal to Liberty as well as Democracy.

In short the current functioning of Economy in Bhaarat (being copied from Western Economists) should not be continued for closing the gap between Rich & Poor is what Shri Mitchell is suggesting.

With regards,

— — — Mukund Apte
