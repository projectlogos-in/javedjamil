#### A Systematic Study of the Holy Qur’an — Part IV — The System

![](https://javedjamilhome.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/09/a1f56-199fnyvtvlbahqmm0a_9rhq.jpeg?w=736)  

In Islam, **spiritual elevation is not an isolated discipline**; it is the part of the overall system aimed at achieving peace at all levels. Islam **does not promote isolationism or monasticism.** It does not support such spiritual exercises as are bereft of responsibility towards family and society. Spiritual exercises in Islam are aimed at **building character**; and character-building in the sight of God is **not limited to an individual; the character of the family and that of society are of equal importance**. The spiritual exercises primarily involve **_Ehsan_** _meaning the_ **_Best Conduct_**_,_ **_Taqwa_** that means **utmost alertness to act only within the boundaries provided by the_Shariah_**, **control of _Nafs_** so that it does not fall victim to the vicissitudes of the corrupt world, and concern for others only for the Pleasure (**_Ridha_**) of God. One cannot become a true _Momin_ until one achieves **gnosis (_Maarifah_ or _Irfan_**) of God and His Messenger. **Gnosis applies to a specific stage of knowledge which is attained not just through studies and researches but also through personal experiences attained through total involvement.** The real _Iman_ is not just the declaration of faith in God and His Messengers; but **obeying them in every sphere of life.** For this purpose one has to follow the examples set by the Messengers, particularly Muhammad, the Last Messenger, his **_Ahl-e-Bayt_** and his **_Sahaba_** (True Companions).

**In Islam, every follower is expected to work for his or her spiritual ascent which is as much achieved through the realisation of the Rights of Creatures (_Huquq-al-Ibad_) as much of the Rights of the Creator (_Huquq-Allah_). Rights of Creatures include not only the rights of human beings in different positions, but also the rights of inanimate creatures like water, air, animals, plants and other components of environment. If one wants to raise one’s spiritual level, one has to be righteous in deeds. One must be courteous in behaviour, give as much in charity as one can (not for fame but for the pleasure of God), encourage others to perform virtuous deeds and avoid all forms of evils, help the week, the needy and the downtrodden, not get involved in any of the undesirable acts; shun violence, not speak ill of others, not boast, not feel arrogant, not preach hatred, not believe in superstitions, and so on. To develop high moral standards, one must concentrate on prayers; prayers and invocations soften the heart, purify the soul and revitalise the spirit so that one develops internal aversion for evils and inclination towards virtues as one comes under God’s special protection. In short, spiritualism in Islam is the other name of internal revolution that ultimately leads to one’s determination and resolve to join external revolution.**

### Spiritual and Moral System in The Qur’an

#### Models

People committed (to Islam)! Follow God, His Ambassador and the authority among you. If you have any internal disputes, refer it to God and His Ambassador, if you are committed (to God) and the Last Day. (4: 59)

Members of the Family! Certainly God intends to expel every kind of sin from you people, and to make you perfectly pure. (33: 33)

There are the persons who took lead (in the Islamic Movement) from among the Migrants (of Makkah) and the Helpers (of Madinah) and the persons who imitated them with excellence. God is pleased with them and they are pleased with God; for them God has in store Gardens, in lower areas of which rivers flow, as their permanent abode. That is the truly great success. (9: 100/A)

(Note: Companions include Muhajirs (who left Mecca for Islam) and Ansar (Helpers), the people of Medina who helped Muhajirs settle there).

#### True righteousness

Virtuousness is not that you turn your faces eastwards or westwards. On the contrary, (the man of) true virtuousness is one who honestly commits himself to God, the Last Day, the Executive (Angels), the (Final) Testament, and the Ambassadors; expends wealth, out of His love, for the nears, the orphans, the needy, the wayfarer, the seekers of help and for ransoming; organises Blessing (Salat), and regularly gives clearance-dues (Zakah). Such people fulfill the agreements they have made, and remain patient in times of sufferings and losses and disaster. They are the people who conduct rightly; and they are the Duteous People. (2:177)

Further, there is a category of the people who barter even their lives to earn the pleasure of God; and God is Kind to (His) devotees. (2: 207)

#### Peace, not evil

People who commit themselves! Take admission in the System of Peace (Islam) in a complete way and do not tread the footsteps of the Satan; surely he is your declared enemy. (2:208)

Is there any reward for excellence except excellence (in return)? (55: 60)

#### Perseverance

State: Should I tell you about something much better than these (worldly things)? For the people who remain on their guard to God there are in store with their Lord the Gardens with rivers flowing underneath them, as their permanent place of living in the company of their spotless spouses and along with the Pleasure of God. And God has in sight long-term (favours) on the people obedient to Him. They are the people who implore: “Our Lord! We are indeed committed to you. So, give us amnesty from our sins, and save us from the agony of the Fire.” They are People of Perseverance, honest, obedient, and generous in charity and seekers of amnesty (from God) in the early morning hours. (3: 15–17)

#### Sacrifices important

You cannot achieve real goodness of character unless you develop (the habit of) spending out of what you hold as dear to you; and whatever you spend, God is aware of it. (3: 92)

#### Help the Needy

Worship God exclusively without ever including any (deity) other than Him (in worship). Conduct with excellence with your parents, relatives, orphans, needy, close neighbours, distant neighbours, colleagues, travellers and your slaves. (4: 36)

There is no charge against the persons who have (recently) committed (to Islam) and are now performing nicely for what they consumed in the past, provided they remain on their guard (to God) and committed (to Islam) and keep performing well. (5: 93)

They are the ones who keep their commitments, and are worried about a day which will have widespread chaos; and purely out of love, serve food to the needy, the orphans and the prisoners telling them “We serve food to you only for the attention of God, and we do not want anything in return, even thanks, from you.” (76: 7–9)

#### Charity

And be proper in positioning for Blessings (Salat) and pay Clearance-dues: And whatever good you send ahead for yourselves, you are going to find it (stored) in the Presence of God: certainly God watches all that you do. (2: 110)

Further, expend of in the cause of God and do not use your hands towards your destruction; conduct yourselves in an excellent way; God loves the people of excellence. (2: 195)

The example of the people who spend their wealth in the cause of God is a grain of corn: it multiplies into seven ears, and each ear in turn has hundreds of grains; and God multiplies manifold for whom He determines. And God is Facilitator-Prescient. There are people, who spend their wealth in the cause of God, and after that, they neither make a show of their gratitude nor abuse the (recipients) in any way; their Prize is in store with their Lord; they will have nothing to worry or despair about. A statement of appreciation and courtesy is better than charity followed by abuse; and God is Self-Sufficient-Lenient. People with commitment! Do not spoil your charities by repeated mentioning of your generosity or by traumatising (the recipients in any way), similar to the people who spend their wealth for outward display (of piety) in society, not out of honest commitment to God and the Last Day. So their example is that of a hard, barren rock, with a little soil: when rain falls on it, it leaves it a bare stone. They will be in no position to benefit from what they have earned. And God does not lead the Community of Dissenters to Guidance. Moreover, the example of the people, who spend their wealth to earn the pleasure of God and with the strength (of commitment) in their souls, is that of a garden, situated on a raised land and fertile: when rain falls abundantly on it, it yields redoubled harvest, and even if it does not receive abundant rain, light watering is enough for it; and God watches all your actions. (2: 261–265)

If you openly give charities, it is alright; and if you secretly give to the needy, it is more appropriate for you: It will alleviate from you some of your weaknesses. And God has complete knowledge about whatever you do. (2: 271)

Moreover, surely, the men and women who engage in charitable activities and in the process give an excellent loan to God will have their loans credited back to them multiplied; and there will be for them a generous reward in addition. (57: 18)

If something excellent embraces you, it distresses them, but if something bad troubles you, they rejoice. If you maintain perseverance, their machinations cannot harm you; surely, God encompasses all their activities. (3: 120)

#### Gentle dealing

So it is with the beneficence of God that you are soft towards them; and if you had been rude or harsh by heart, they would have distanced themselves from your circle. So, forgive them (for their faults) and ask for God’s amnesty to them. (3: 159)

#### Hypocrisy condemned

Consequent upon their violation of the covenant of God and recurrent lies, God afflicted their hearts with scepticism until the Day comes when they will face Him. (9: 77)

Prophet! Remain on your guard to God and do not follow the Dissenters and the Hypocrites. Surely, God is Prescient-Wise. (33: 1)

The men committed (to Islam) are honest in their allegiance to God. Among them there are ones who have already fulfilled their promises, and others are waiting (for the right time); and they have no intention to change. This is in the hope that God will reward the honest for their honesty, and will punish the Sceptics, subject to His Determination, or will accept their apology accompanied with the pledge not to repeat (their mistakes again). Surely, God is Forgiving-Merciful. (33: 24)

If the Hypocrites, those whose hearts are sick (with scepticism) and those who spread revolt in the City, do not refrain (their misconduct), We will surely ask you to rise against them. Following this, they will not be able to stay in your vicinity any longer. (33: 60)

When they are invited to the (Book) dispatched by God and to the Ambassador, you will find the Hypocrites (sceptics) turn away their faces from you in disdain. (4: 61)

Announce in advance to the Hypocrites (sceptics) that a deadly punishment is in store for them. (4: 138)

Of course, the Sceptics try to deceive God, but (in truth) He keeps them in deception. When they stand for Blessing (Prayer), they do so without sincerity, only to make a show to the people and they hardly think of God. They are in a state of confusion in their minds neither being on the side of one group (of devout Muslims) nor on the side of the (misguided) people. Moreover, you cannot show way to anyone left unguided by God. (4: 142–143)

Certainly, the Hypocrites will be in the midst of the worst parts of the Fire; you will not find anyone helping them. (4: 145)

#### Avoid sins

Moreover, if anyone procures a sin, he procures it at the cost of himself; and God is Prescient-Wise. (4: 111)

And do not be an advocate of the dishonest; instead, seek forgiveness from God; surely, God is Forgiving-Merciful. Moreover, do not indulge in an argument on behalf of those who keep themselves in deception; surely God does not love the persons engaged in sinful, perfidious behaviour. (4: 105–107)

#### No conspiracies

Hardly anything beneficial comes out in most of their secret meetings with the exception of an occasional participant who advocates charity, welfare or reform within the mankind. (4: 114)

Goes does not like the evil (of anyone) being publicly noised, except by anyone who has been seriously offended. God alone is the Learned-Court (4: 148)

#### No impudence

State: My Lord has prohibited nothing but the illicit relations, open as well as secret, the (known) sins and the violations without any mandate. (7: 33)

#### No disputes

Further, remain obedient to God and His Ambassador; and do not fall into mutual disputes, lest you lose heart and your strength depart. Further, keep patience! (8: 46)

#### No mischief

Why among the previous generations were there none with a sense to lead campaign against creating turmoil on the earth with the exception of a few whom We gave salvation? Further, the offenders pursued the worldly benefits they got (in a wrong way) and were proven criminals. (11: 116)

Moreover, there are persons who breach the Covenant of God, after solemnising it, and create schism in violation of God’s injunctions, spreading turmoil on the earth; they are cursed upon and there is in store for them an ugly place of living. (13: 25)

Moreover, do not give up the Covenant of God in return of a small amount; what is with God is far superior. (One wish) you knew it! (16: 95)

Consume the things God has provided you, which are lawful and healthy; and remain on your guard to God the One you are committed to. (5: 88)

#### No oaths for bad purposes

Moreover, do not make resolves to deceive your own people with the purpose that someone standing firm on his feet falls flat; this putting obstacles in the path of God for others would lead to bad consequences for you as well, and (above all) a deadly punishment will be in store for you (in the Hereafter). (16: 94)

#### Neither a miser nor a spendthrift

Neither tie your hands to your necks (not spending at all) nor stretch them so much that you are accused (of wasting) and become a destitute yourself. (17: 29)

Moreover, you must give your relatives what they deserve as their right, and (charities) to the needy and travellers (in need) but take care not to overspend like a squanderer. Certainly, squanderers are brothers of the devil; and the devil is a dissenter against his Lord. (17: 26–27)

Further, do not pursue something you have no knowledge about. Certainly, ears, eyes and hearts will all be questioned (in Hereafter about their roles). (17: 36)

#### Avoid superstitions

They ask you about the New Moons. Tell them that they serve as a calendar for the people and for determining the days of Pilgrimage. Furthermore, there is no virtuousness in entering your houses from the back; the vituousness on the other hand is in being dutiful. Enter your houses through their doors; and remain on your guard to God so that you may succeed. (2: 189)

Moreover, they assign for God a share from the produce and the cattle He has grown for them and then boastfully declare: “This is for God, and this is for our partners!” Subsequently, while the share of their partners does not reach God, the share of God (too) reaches their partners. Bad is what they scheme. (6: 136)

Moreover, they make unfounded claims about specific animals and plant products being forbidden, and that none can eat them except what they declare themselves as Approved; further, (according to their claims), there are cattle whose backs cannot be used (for carrying the load) and there are cattle that are not allowed to be (slaughtered) in the name of God. These are nothing but false claims on His behalf; subsequently He will give them the deserving return for their false claims. Moreover, they claim that the yet-to-be-born embryos of certain specific animals are reserved for their men but forbidden for their women; but if it happens to be stillborn all (men as well women) have share (of blame). He will give them their deserving return for their (false) specifications. (6: 138–139/A)

Similarly, their extra-gods have made the sacrifice of their children an attractive proposition for the purpose of their destruction with their own hands and creating confusion in their minds about their religious system. If God had not given them freedom (of action), they could not have done it. So (you also) ignore them and the distortions they make. (6: 137)

#### No boasting, no arrogance

Of course, it is easy for God; it means that you should not feel unduly sad about losses, and should not be unduly jubilant over your gains; and (remember that) God does not love boastful, arrogant persons. They are the people who are greedy and advocate greediness in human society. Moreover, anyone who deviates (should know) that God is Rich-Glorified. (57: 22–24)

Do not walk on the earth in an arrogant manner; certainly you cannot tear away the earth, nor can you grow taller in height than mountains. (17: 37)

Moreover, do not swell your cheeks (in arrogance) in the midst of the people and do not walk on the earth in an arrogant manner. Surely, God does not like any proud boaster. (31: 18)

#### No slanders

Moreover, those who cause harm to men and women committed (to Islam) without any reason laden only themselves with the charge of defamation and a big sin. (33: 58)

The reason for contempt is only against those who oppress the people and tyrannise the earth without any mandate. A terrible punishment is in store for such people. (42: 42)

#### No laughing at others

People of Commitment! One group of men should not make fun of other group of men, as the latter may possibly be worthier than the former. (Similarly), a group of women should not make fun of other women, as the latter may possibly be worthier than the former. Moreover, do not speak ill of one another, nor call others by offensive titles. (49: 11)

#### Suspiciousness disliked

People Committed (to Islam)! Avoid guesswork as much (as possible) because some guesses may well be (in the category of) sins. Moreover, do not spy against one another nor backbite. Would anyone like to eat the flesh of his dead brother? (Of course), you would abhor it. 49: 12)

Blown be the falsehood-mongers! They are desperately (drowned in) in a flood of confusion. (51: 10–11)

#### Avoid shamelessness

There are people who keep themselves away from major sins and indecencies committing only some minor faults. Your Lord is surely Extensively Forgiving. He knew you (men) when he first produced you as a new species from the Earth, and when you were foetuses inside your mothers’ wombs. So, do not try to appear pure. He knows who is dutiful (and who is not). Do you not look at the persons who turn backwards, giving a little (in charity) and then toughening (their attitude)? (53: 32–34)

Further, they refuted (the Truth) and followed their own fantasies; and there is a final destination for every function. (54: 3)

(When you give in charity) do not do it expecting any increase in your (wealth); rather do it for your Lord; subsequently keep patience. (74: 6–7)

Have you noticed one who refutes the System (of God)? Consequently, he is the one who asks the orphan to go away, and does not promote the meals to the needy. Hence, curse is on the people who do pray, but are careless about (the essence of) the prayers, (praying) more for public view while denying help to the needy. (107)

#### Weigh correctly

Subsequently, to the (people of) Madyan (We sent) one of their brothers, Shuayb. He addressed to (them): “My Community! Worship God; there is none worth worshipping other than Him. Further, do not deceitfully reduce the weight or measurement. I find you in prosperity here but I am worried about the Torment that will (ultimately) surround you. My Community! Give full deserving amount in measure and weight, and do not withhold the items (of consumption) from the common people, and do not commit evils on the earth, becoming involved in corrupt practices. (11: 84–85)

#### Do not pollute the environment

And there is the sky (the first cosmic tier) which He extended to great heights establishing equilibrium (in it) so that you may not disturb the equilibrium. (55: 7–8)

#### Fraudulence really bad

Curse on those who are dishonest in dealings: they exact full amount when they receive, and when they give, they cut the amount short! (83: 1–3)

#### Polytheism never

There came a time when during a sermon to his son Luqman addressed to him thus: “My son! Do not include in worship anyone other than God. Certainly pantheism is the greatest offence.” (31: 13/A)

**_(_**Luqman continued): “My son! Certainly, if there is something even of the weight of mustard seed hidden in a rock or anywhere in the Cosmos or in the Earth, God will bring it out. He surely has the information about the finest mysteries. My son! Organise Blessings (prayers), promote what is admirable, and campaign against what is forbidden and maintain perseverance whatever befalls you. Certainly, this is the (true nature of) firm determination in the affairs (of life). Further, do not swell your cheeks at fellow human beings, nor walk insolently on the earth. Without doubt, God does not love any arrogant boaster. Further, keep moderation in your speed and low intensity in your voice. Surely, the most disagreeable sound is that of ass.” (31: 16–19)

#### Be good to parents

Further, We have entrusted man with (the responsibility of) his parents; his mother kept his burden (in her womb); and bearing one stress after the other his weaning took (another) two years. Be thankful to Me and your parents! To me is your Destination. Further, if the (parents) make efforts to make you indulge in Inclusive worship of entities you have no knowledge about, do not obey them; still live along with them in this life in an admirable manner. Further, imitate the method of those who turn in devotion towards Me. Following this (life), you all have to return back to Me, and I will let you know the truth of whatever you did (in the world). (31: 14’15)

#### Purification of soul

“Moreover, I do not absolve my inner self: of course the Inner Self is inclined towards the evil, except when my Lord bestows His Mercy (on it). Undoubtedly my God is Forgiving-Merciful.” (12: 53)

Further, I swear by the Inner Self of Conscience. (75: 2)

“O Satisfied Inner Self! Return back to your Lord, pleased (with Him) and in (His) Pleasure, and join the company of My devout followers!” (89: 27–29)

Further, whoever submits with his whole attention, and is an excellent performer in respect to God has surely grasped the most dependable Rope; and towards God are all the matters destined. (31: 22)

#### Work for God

You enwrapped in garments! Do stand (for Blessings) in the night except for a small part of it — half or a little less than that, or a little more than that, and recite Quran in a proper, pleasant style! We are surely going to transmit to you a Heavy Statement. The awakening in the night is a potent exercise for meditating and organizing the speech indeed. For you the day is certainly a long period of hectic movement. Further, always keep in thought the Name of your Lord and devote yourself entirely to Him! (73: 1–8)

#### Remembrance of God

Of course, your Lord knows it well that you, and a group of your companions, stand (for Blessings) from nearly two thirds of the night, to half or one third of it. Moreover, God keeps changing the duration of the day and the night. He knows that you are not in a position to keep an exact calculation of this (variation); He has therefore given kind attention to you. Hence, (from now onward) recite as much of Quran as is easy for you! He understands that some of you may be on rest due to an ailment; others busy (somewhere) on the earth in pursuit of God’s grant; and still many engaged in fighting for the cause of God. Hence, recite the (Quran) as much as is easy for you, organize Blessings and pay your Purification Dues, and (in the process) give a loan of excellence to God! And whatever acts of quality you send ahead for yourselves, you will find it in store with God, greatly increased in dividend. In addition, ask for Amnesty of God! God is surely Forgiving-Merciful. (73: 20)

People committed (to God)! Stand firm in the (cause of) God, as just witnesses, and do not allow the hatred for a certain community make you commit an offence in violation of justice. Remain committed to justice, which is close to guarded dutifulness. Surely, God is fully cognizant of whatever you do. (5: 8)

Moreover, meditate your Lord with the depth of your soul, with humility and awe, without openly uttering, in the mornings and evenings; and do not be forgetful! Those of you, who are close to your Lord, are not arrogant towards His worship. They are active in glorifying Him, and bow to Him (in submission). (7: 205–206)

There are people who have committed (to Islam) and find solace in meditation to God; undoubtedly the satisfaction of hearts lies in the meditation to God. The people who commit and engage in virtuous deeds are (surely) in a state of blessedness and are destined to have a pleasant return. (13: 28–29)

There are people who donate their wealth for the sake of self-purification, not in hope of any favour being returned in kind, only expecting the attention of their Highest Lord, and they will (of course) be bestowed with (His) Pleasure. (92: 18–21)

#### God’s pleasure

There are people who behave with patient determination, seeking the Attention of their Lord, expend out of what We have granted them, openly as well as secretly, and counter the bad with the excellence; for them is (is in store) the (bliss of) Final Home. (13: 22)

Time is witness that mankind has been in a state of loss, with the exception of those who are committed (to God), engage in virtuous deeds, propagate the Mandate, and propagate it with patient determination. (103: 1–3)
