> When the galaxy of Westernism appeared on the horizon, “Freedom of Choice” was one of its most shining stars. Within two centuries it has become a black hole; it was destined to burn out soon because it was designed and is still being used to commercialise not only human needs and strengths but also human susceptibilities. Westernism with its concepts of unlimited “freedom” including “freedom of choice” has engulfed almost the entire world including the Independent India, which slowly and steadily has fallen to the domination of the Corporate. The tragedy is that the nation hardly ever debates the kind of freedom it has been subjected to by its masters……This strategy of Normalization, Institutionalisation, Legalisation and Commercialisation was adopted in the case of alcohol, gambling, prostitution and pornography and continues to be the mainstay in the globalisation of homosexuality. The demoniac march of commercialisation has necessitated that all the vices and perversions must be considered “normal and natural” behaviour and any stigma associated with them should be struck with a fatal blow..
