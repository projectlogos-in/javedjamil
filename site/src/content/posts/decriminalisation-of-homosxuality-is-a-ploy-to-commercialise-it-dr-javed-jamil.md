#### By Muslim Mirror News (December 22, 2013)

![](https://javedjamilhome.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/09/e6ff1-0cikkvlxtuye3shuh.jpg?w=736)  

Renowned thinker and analyst, Dr Javed Jamil has said that **_“Attempts of decriminalisation are nothing but a ploy to commercialise homosexuality.”_** He was delivering a special lecture on the implications of Supreme Court judgment on section 377 at JIH Headquarters at New Delhi. He said that the current world ruled by “the forces of economic fundamentalism” seeks to commercialise every “human susceptibility”, and the global movement to legalise homosexuality is aimed at the same goal. He asked “when for the last time a person was charged under section 377” and if there is no case pending against any homosexual under this section, why there is such an urgency to decriminalise the activity. Obviously, the forces of economics are interested in decriminalisation because like Western countries where homosexuality is the biggest growing industry among all sex industries, they find India a country of high potential. He said that this has been an old method of the forces of economic fundamentalism to first try to “normalise an evil, then institutionalise and legalise it and once the legalisation has been achieved, to commercialise it,” Once, legalisation is achieved, in India too, like West, the gay clubs, gay hotels, gay resorts, gay pornography, gay literature, gay counselling centres and gay marriage and divorce bureaus will mushroom.

Dr Jamil systematically countered the arguments put forward by the proponents of gay rights. He said that **_“there is a huge difference between ensuring human rights of all criminals including homosexuals and accepting human sexuality as a human right.”_** He said that **_“the argument of accepting homosexuality on account of its existing since old times is ludicrous because all crimes including murders, thefts, rapes and promiscuity have existed since the oldest times.”_** He also countered the argument of **_“consent of two individuals”_** by saying that **_“in that case even bribery and sex determination tests should be legalised since they are in most cases related to the consent of two or three individuals out of their freedom of choice”_**. He also lambasted the notion that homosexuality is natural by saying that “there are no conclusive medical proofs regarding that and even if this is accepted as a congenital problem, these should be treated like all congenital problems.”

Dr Jamil advocated coming together of all religions against vices including homosexuality. He expressed dismay at the ambivalent approach of Christian clerics who condemn homosexuality as a sin but refuse to accept it as crime. He explained that sins are the acts that do not damage anyone else while crimes are those acts that have the potential of causing damage to others. **_“There cannot be a bigger crime than homosexuality because it threatens the very survival of human species and is the biggest risk factor in spread of sex diseases including AIDS.”_** He told that the average life expectancy has been shown to be 15–25 years lesser than the normal people. He asked the international institutions why they are so much unconcerned about the deaths caused by huge industrialisation of sex. He said that “Sexual Revolution has already killed more than 40 million people from AIDS and the sexuality promoted by the New World Order has sopped at least 1 billion humans from being born because they have been killed by way of abortion.” He asked why only the terrorism related deaths which are only few thousands has attracted world attention and “war against terror” killing 2 millions in the last decade.

Dr Jamil lambasted the “apologetic and defensive” approach of Muslim intellectuals and media and called for a total ideological onslaught against the New World Order. He said that mere defence of Islam and Muslims would not be enough and Muslim institutions will have to wage a counter attack against false ideologies, concepts, programmes and policies. He exhorted Muslims to ally with other religions in this fight. **_“Islam is for the peace, safety and security of the mankind, and Muslims must not simply pay lip service to this mission but must aggressively pursue this through concerted efforts on all fronts.”_** He said. He also called for a campaign to introduce “Right to Health” as a fundamental right in the constitution and inclusion of “Fundamental Prohibitions” along with “Fundamental Rights” and “Fundamental Duties”.

The lecture was attended among others by Maulana Jalal Umri, Ammer of Jamaat-e Islami-e Hind and Mr Mujtaba Farooq, President of Welfare Party.
