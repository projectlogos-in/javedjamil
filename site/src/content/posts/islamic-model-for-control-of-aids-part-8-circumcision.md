#### **Circumcision**

This was in 1997 that I had authored “Islamic Model for Control of AIDS”. Though there were a few reports of the possible relationship between circumcision and protection from AIDS at that time, I was perhaps the first in the world to advocate the inclusion of circumcision as part of the AIDS Control Programme. Following the publication of that book, PEACE, an NGO that I founded, had organised a three day seminar on “In Search of a Comprehensive Solution for AIDS” in collaboration with National AIDS Control Organisation, Ministry of Health & Family Welfare of the Government of India. In this seminar too, I had argued in favour of the inclusion of circumcision in the NACO programme. The proceedings of that seminar were published in 1999. But NACO did never take the recommendations of the seminar seriously. It was after about 8–9 years of the demand first made by me that the WHO and other organisations started contemplating to include circumcision as part of the strategy to prevent AIDS. Some other aspects of circumcision have also been discussed elsewhere in the book.
