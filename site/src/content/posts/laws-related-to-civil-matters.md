#### A Systematic Study of the Holy Qur’an — Part III — Constitution

![](https://javedjamilhome.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/09/8b914-1e67sw2qof24e6scmgadrtw.jpeg?w=736)  

### **1.** **Deals, contracts and property**  

#### **In Writing**

People with commitment! When you make transactions for a fixed period of time, put them into writing. Let a notary honestly note down everything agreed upon by the parties: the notary must not refuse to write the way God has taught him; so he must put into writing what he is dictated by the liable party; and he must remain on his guard to his Lord God and must not make any concessions. (2: 282)

Do not shy away from putting into writing (your contract), small or big, agreed upon for the fixed time. To God it is nearer to justice, more appropriate in establishing evidence and more effective in mimimising disputes among you. (2: 282)

If the liable party is mentally deficient, or weak, or unable to dictate itself, its attorney must dictate honestly. ..(2: 282)

Further, if it is an on the spot transaction between you, there is nothing wrong on your part if you do not put it into writing. (2: 282)

If you are in the midst of a journey and cannot find a scribe, make a pledge about the possession. Moreover, if one of you deposits something on trust to another person, the trustee must honestly discharge his trust, and he must remain on his guard to his Lord. (2: 283)

Furthermore, get two witnesses, out of your own men, and if two men are not there, get a man and two women of your choice as witnesses; so that if one of them deviates from their correct position, the other reminds her. (2: 282)

#### Witnesses should oblige

The witnesses should not refuse (to give evidence) when they are called. (2: 282)

The notary must not refuse to write the way God has taught him; so he must put into writing what he is dictated by the liable party; and he must remain on his guard to his Lord God and must not make any concessions. (2: 282)

Do not hide the evidence; so, whoever conceals it, his heart becomes sinful. And God knows whatever you do. (2: 283)

Furthermore, take witness whenever you make a commercial contract; (2: 282)

…and let neither the scribe nor the witness suffer on account of it; If you do that it would be wickedness on your part.(2: 282)

#### Trusts to be safeguarded

God of course enjoins you to give back the trusts to the persons who are their real owners….(4: 58)

#### No Wrongdoing

Moreover, do not consume each other’s wealth in an unjustified manner, nor use it to bribe the judges so that you may consume some part of the wealth of other people in a sinful way; and that too knowingly.(2: 188)

Be honest in measure and weight; do not withhold from the people the things that are theirs. (7: 85)

#### Orphans’ properties protected

Moreover, hand over to orphans their assets as such, without substituting good things with bad; and do not devour their assets by adding them to your own. (4: 2)

Moreover, keep orphans under strict supervision till they become mature for marriage. Then, if you find them on the right track, hand over their assets to them. Moreover, do not do not consume it beyond requirement nor show eagerness (in using as much as possible) before they grow up. If the (guardian) is rich, he should avoid claiming (any charges), and if he is poor, he may consume what is reasonable. Then when you hand over to them their assets, do it in the presence of witnesses; (4: 6)

Moreover, if they turn out to be fools, do not hand over to them their assets, which God has made means of support for you, but fulfil their food and clothing requirements, and speak with them in an appreciable way. (4: 5)

### **_2\. Inheritance_**

#### **Share for both men and women**

From what is left by parents and those nearest related there is a share for men and a share for women…(4: 7/)

But if at the time of division other relatives, or orphans or poor, are present, arrange food for them out of the (assets), and speak to them words of kindness and justice. (4:8)

These are God’s directions regarding your children: to the males a share equal to that of two females: if (one has) only daughters, two or more, their share is two thirds of the total inheritance; if only one, her share is half; for parents in case the deceased has left children, each one gets one sixth share; if there are no children left, and the parents are the only heirs, the mother has one third; if the deceased left brothers (or sisters), the mother has one sixth. (The distribution is to be made) after the payment of all dues and debts. (4: 11)

#### Share of husbands and children

If the man or woman whose inheritance is in question, has left neither ascendants nor descendants, but has left a brother or a sister, each one of the two gets a sixth; but if more than two, they share in a third; after payment of legacies and debts; so that no loss is caused (to any one). (4: 12/)

To those, also, to whom your right hand was pledged, give their due portion. (4: 33/A)

Further, to those also with whom you are bound by an oath, give their due share. (4: 33)

### **_3\. Wills_**

#### **Will desirable**

It is ordained to you that when death becomes imminent for any of you, in case he is going to leave behind any assets, he must make a will for parents and relatives, in accordance with the known procedure; this is mandatory on the dutiful.(2: 180)

If anyone changes the will after hearing it, its sin goes in the account of the persons who change it. God is Hearer-Prescient indeed. (2: 181)

#### For Widows

Those of you who die and leave widows should bequeath for their widows a year’s maintenance and residence…(2: 240)

#### Witnesses for bequests

People Committed (to Islam)! When death stares anyone of you, (you should take) witnesses from among yourselves at the time of making bequests — two men committed to justice from among your close ones or, if the danger of death suddenly overtakes you while you are travelling in a (distant) part of the earth, from among outsiders. If you have any doubts about their intentions, hold both of them after the Prayer (of Blessings) and ask them to swear by God (like this): “We will not sell (our oaths) for any paltry price, even if it benefits any of our relatives; and we will not conceal the testimony of God; if we do, we will be bringing sin upon us.” Subsequently, if there is information coming about the two committing the sin (of perjury) ask other two to stand in their place, from among the closest to the claimants of a lawful right. 5: 106–108)

#### Adopted children not equal to natural inheritors

God did not form two hearts inside human body; nor he made women divorced by Zihar\* your real mothers; nor he made your adopted sons your real (biological) sons. (33: 4)

\* a former practice of divorce by calling his wife her mother.

_(Note: Islam does not give adopted children the same rights as the real ones. The near relatives have bigger right to inherit than the adopted ones. But one is always free to make a will about one third of one’s property which one can bequeath to anyone including adopted children.)_

### **_4\. Booty_**

Moreover, be informed that out of every part of the booty captured in a war, one fifth share is assigned to God, the Ambassador, close relatives, orphans, the needy and the stranded travellers. .(8:41)

Moreover, what God delivered to the Ambassador from the residents of the towns, is (to be assigned) for the cause of God, Ambassador, his relatives, orphans, the needy and the stranded travellers. This is to ensure that the wealth is not distributed only within the rich among you. Moreover, (be satisfied) in taking what the Ambassador gives you and keep yourselves away from what he denies you. And remain on the guard to God; He is strict in the punishment. Moreover, there is (share in booty) also for the poor immigrants who abandoned their homes and assets seeking only God’s grants and pleasure, supporting (the cause of) God and His Ambassador. (59: 7–8)

### **_5\. Oaths_**

God does not hold you responsible for what was insincere in your oaths. But He holds you responsible for your oaths taken with honest intention: for the purpose of expiation (following breach of oath) in such cases, you have to provide ten poor persons meals of a quality, which you provide to your family on an average; or give them clothes to wear or give freedom to one slave. If you are not in a position to do any of these, you have to fast for three consecutive days. (5: 89)

### **_6\. Marriage and dower_**

Arrange marriage of the persons who are single among you, as well as of your slaves, male and female, who are of good character. If they are poor, God will fulfil their requirements through His grant; and God is All-Encompassing-Prescient. (24: 32)

Moreover, the persons who are not in a financial position to marry must safeguard themselves (against any illicit relationship) till God fulfils their requirements through His Grant. (24: 33)

Next; it is no crime on your part if you express your desire to marry the widows or you keep it in your hearts. God knows that you will think and talk of them; now, do not make a secret promise to them till you pronounce the declaration (of marriage) in accordance with the recognised procedure; and do not determine the tie of marriage till the book of their term (with their dead husbands) closes. And know that God knows all that is in your hearts, and remain on your guard for Him; and know that God is Forgiving- Lenient. (2: 235)

#### Polygyny allowed but not preferred

Marry women of your choice, two or three or four, but if you are worried that you will not be able to deal with them equitably, then (marry) only one or a woman under your possession. (4: 3)

You may never be able to be exactly equal in treatment with your wives, even if you seriously desire; but make sure that you do not abandon a particular wife altogether. (4: 129)

#### Dower

Moreover, present to women (at the time of marriage) free gifts; now if on their own they return any part of it to you, have it and cheerfully enjoy it. (4: 4)

Except the mentioned ones, all others are lawful to you on the condition that you seek (marriage with) them in return of assets, for a healthy relationship not for temporary pleasure; now as you derive benefits from them, paying them adequate returns for their (functions) is your duty. There is no charge against you if there is a (change) with mutual agreement. (4: 24)

Marry them with the permission of their owners, and give them their dues in accordance with the known procedure. (4: 25)

### **_7\. Divorce and related matters_**

#### Reconciliation

If you are worried about a rift between the (husband and wife), appoint arbiters from his people and (similar) arbiters from her family; if they wish to improve (their relations), God will bring reconciliation between them. (4: 35)

#### Abstention

For the people who turn away from their wives, a limit of four months is fixed; so if they choose to return, God is Forgiving-Merciful. (2: 226)

#### Divorce before consummation

Next; it is no crime on your part if you divorce women before you touch them or before fixing their obligatory (dower); and (in that case) pay them: the well-to-do according to his capability, and the poor according to his capability. A payment in accordance with the recognised procedure is mandated on the People of Excellence. (2: 236)

Next; if you divorce them before you touch them, but after fixing their obligatory payment, they will have half of the dower, unless they remit it or is remitted by the person who arranged this marriage tie; and to remit is closer to guarded dutifulness (to God). And do not forget to be gracious with one another. Surely God watches all your actions. (2: 237)

#### Iddah

People committed (to Islam)! When you marry women committed (to Islam), and then divorce them before having physical contact with them, you need not count a period of Iddah (Waiting) in respect to them. Subsequently, present them an amount (as a parting gift), and give them freedom in a pleasant manner. (33: 49)

**Prophet! When you people have to divorce women, divorce them for the Iddah (Waiting Period); keep a record of Iddah, and remain on your guard to God, your Lord. (During Iddah), do not banish them from their living places, nor are they allowed to leave on their own, except where there is an open display of indecency on their part. These are Divine limits; and anyone who crosses the Divine limits offends his own self. You cannot (often) foresee some new developments that God can cause to happen after this pronouncement (of divorce). Subsequently, when the time of their (Iddah) is mature, you either hold them in accordance with the procedure or part with them in accordance with the procedure. (65: 1–2)**

#### Procedure of divorce and related matters

.. Moreover, take two persons, capable of acting with justice, as witness from among you, and establish the testimony for the sake of God. (65: 2)

#### Period of Iddah

Divorced women will bind themselves for three monthly periods. (2: 228)

If you have any doubts regarding the women who have entered the age of menopause, the period of Iddah (waiting) is three months, the (same being ) for the women having amenorrhoea (for any other reason). (65: 4)

For those who are burdened (with pregnancy), their period is till they get free of their burden. (65: 4)

#### Annulment during Iddah

Moreover, it is not right for them to conceal what God has created in their wombs, if they are committed to God and the Last Day; and in that period their husbands have the right to annul the divorce, if they decide to reconcile. (2: 228)

#### Treatment during Iddah

Make arrangement for living for them where you live, according to your means, and do not annoy them (in any way) in order to pressurise them. Moreover, if they are pregnant, spend on them till they get completely free of their burden. Following that, if they breastfeed them, (it is your duty to) give them an adequate remuneration through a mutual consultation in a recognised way. If you have any disputes, let the (child) be breastfed by someone else. (65: 6)

When you divorce women, and their period (of waiting) matures, either retain them in accordance with the recognised procedure or free them in accordance with the recognised procedure; but do not retain them to maltreat them; if any one acts that way he offends himself. Do not make fun of the Arguments of God, and admire Him for His Gift to you in that He dispatched to you the Book and Wisdom that lectures you about everything. And remain dutiful to God, and know that God Knows everything about everything. (2: 231)

#### Iddah for widows

If people in your midst expire leaving behind their wives; the (widows) should keep themselves from remarrying for four months and ten days. Subsequently when they have completed their terms, it is no crime on your part to let them conduct themselves in accordance with the recognised procedure. And God has all the information about what you do. (2: 234)

#### Marriage of divorcees

When you divorce women, and they complete their period of (waiting), do not stop them from marrying their husbands of choice, if they mutually agree in accordance with the recognised procedure. This is an advice for all of you, who are committed to God and the Last Day. That is meant for your cleansing and purification; and God knows what you do not know. (2: 232)

#### Khhula

If a woman is worried about cruelty or neglect on the part of her husband, there is no blame on them if they resolve their (disputes) between themselves; and a mutual resolution (of conflicts) is always better. (4: 128)

#### Maintenance, gifts and provision

The divorce has two stages; remain united in a well known manner or get freedom in an excellent way. It is not right for you, (men), to seize anything that you gave to (your wives), except if both of you worry that you will not be able to maintain the bounds of God. If you are worried that they would not be able to maintain the bounds of God, it is no crime on the part of either if she decides to pay something to earn freedom. They are the bounds of God; so do not cross them. Now, the persons who cross the bounds of God are clear offenders. (2: 229)

For divorced women, there is a provision in accordance with the recognised procedure. This is mandatory on the Dutiful. (2: 241)

Moreover, if you wish to take another wife in place of the existing one, do not take back anything from the latter even if you have given her a whole treasure. Would you seize what would amount to be an insult and a big sin? (4: 20–21)

#### Remarriage after Final Divorce not possible

Once he has finally divorced her, he cannot marry her after this, till she happens to marry another spouse and he divorces her. In that case it is no crime on the part of either of them if they decide to re-unite, provided they strongly feel that they would now maintain the boundaries of God. They are the boundaries of God categorically explained for the knowledge of the nation. (2: 230)

#### Divorce by Zihar

Moreover, the (husbands) who happen to divorce their wives through Zihar and then rue the words they uttered, they must give freedom to a slave before they develop physical contacts again. This is how you are instructed; and God knows what you do. Moreover, if anyone does not have (a slave), he should fast for two months without break before they touch each other. Now if anyone is not in a position to do even this, he should give one time meal to sixty poor persons. (58: 3–4)

_(Note: Zihar was a method of divorce prevalent in the pre-Islamic Arab society, in which the husband used to call his wife as his mother, sister, etc.; if anyone did that, it would signal an end to their relationship.)_
