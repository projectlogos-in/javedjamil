## Current Understanding of Health

A widely accepted definition of health is that of the World Health Organisation, WHO. It states that "health is a state of complete physical, mental and social well-being and not merely the absence of disease or infirmity." This widely accepted definition was expanded in the 1970s and 1980s, as other components were included.

Yet even this expanded definition treats health as a *state* — something to be reached and then defended. The Dynamic Paradigm proposes something different: health as a living process, continuously produced by the ordering of an entire civilisation, not a condition certified once and left alone.

The following instalments in this series work through what that means in practice — for medicine, for economics, and for law.
