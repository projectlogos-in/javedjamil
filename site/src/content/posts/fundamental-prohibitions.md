#### A Systematic Study of the Holy Qur’an

![](https://javedjamilhome.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/09/e0e7e-1c0_2z15hznk5nv9ydc2cqg.png?w=736)  

**As has already been detailed, Islamic Constitution is three-dimensional with equal emphasis on rights, duties and prohibitions. In Islam there is no unlimited freedom of choice. Freedom of choice is limited to choice among the good, pure and healthy things and practices. The choices that endanger life, spread chaos or undue disparity in society or cause harm to self or others are either totally prohibited or restricted depending upon their relative impact. Except for _Shirk_, which is related to spiritual domain, all other prohibitions are related to health, family or social peace.**

The time has now proved that the most glaring deficiency of almost all the prominent systems is that they emphasise only upon fundament rights, underscore fundamental duties and altogether ignore fundamental prohibi­tions. This two-dimensional approach is inadequate in maintaining order in society. Besides, it is inherently dan­gerous, as it unleashes forces of evils and exploitation. No society can maintain order and tranquillity unless it has its sets of restrictions. Its members must not only claim for their own rights, but must also be duty-bound to help in its survival and development and they must not be in a position, even if they want, to do what is expressly detrimental for society. A **three-dimensional** approach is therefore mandatory if the totally paralysed and redundant legal system has to be rejuvenated. The two-dimensional constitu­tions, without any express provisions of fundamental prohibitions, guarantee rights only for the strong and those rights of the weak that the strong seek to exploit. The three-dimensional approach, on the contrary, with explicit Fundamental Prohibitions, is a guarantee for the rights of all the members of society includ­ing the weak and the underprivileged. **Fundamental Prohibitions must be aimed at ensuring the same trio of objectives — Individual Health, Family Peace and Social Order, the three essential components of Grand Peace.**

The modern approach is influenced by economic fundamentalism that seeks to commercialise both _strengths and weaknesses_ of human beings. In order to commercialise weaknesses, it is necessary that “prohibitions” should be totally prohibited and greater emphasis should be given to rights not duties. In Islamic social system, on the other hand, the above-mentioned rights as individual, member of family and member of society are harmonised with duties and prohibitions. **In the edifice of Islamic society, duties form the foundation, rights the roof and prohibition the walls; a building cannot be a building without any of the three, and _all the three_ must be adequately planned and built.**

A system cannot be deemed complete unless it categorically answers three questions: what is one _enjoined_ to do, what is one permitted or _entitled_ to do and what is one _prohibited_ to do? A proper balance between these three is the most wonderful aspect of Islamic constitution that makes it far superior model to the other systems.

### **FUNDAMENTAL PROHIBITIONS ACCORDING TO QURAN**

### **_I) Shirk (Polytheism)_**

Worship God exclusively without ever including any (deity) other than Him (in worship). (4: 36)

### **II)** **Eatables**  

Items prohibited for you are: the meat of the dead (animals), blood, the pork, the meat which has been dedicated to a deity other than God, the flesh of the animals killed by strangulation, or by a violent blow, or by a fall on the head side, or by being gored, or left after hunting by a wild animal, unless you are able to properly slaughter it, or (that of an animal) sacrificed at altars and the (meat) sought through division in (the game of) arrows, which is a violation (of the code). (5: 3)

On the other hand, He has prohibited for you the meat of the dead, the blood, the pork and anything dedicated to deities other than God. But if one is compelled by urgency, without intentional disobedience and transgression, it is no sin for him. God is Forgiving-Merciful indeed. (2:173)

### **III)** **Drinking and gambling**  

They ask you about substances of addiction and gambling. State: “There is huge sin and some benefits for the mankind in them; but the sin is much greater than their benefits.” (2: 219)

People Committed (to Islam)! Substances of addiction, gambling and sacrifices of stones (altars) and (divination) by arrows are dirty Satanic practices. Hence, keep away from them so that you may become successful. Certainly, through substances of addiction and gambling, Satan’s intention is to promote hostility and envy among you people and to create obstacles in your glorification of God and reading Blessings (Salat). (5: 90–91)

### **IV)** **Economic corruption**  

#### **Usury**

People Committed (to Islam)! Do not engulf (the products of) compounded usury; and remain dutiful so that you succeed. (3: 130)

The people who engulf usury cannot have any standing except as ones driven by Satanic touch to madness. This is because they claim that trade is also like usury; and (the truth is that) God has allowed trade and prohibited usury. The people who give up (the practice of usury) after receiving the advice from their Lord, will be given a favourable consideration by God for their past (mistake); the people who continue (with the practice) are going to be the companions of the Fire: They will live there permanently. (2: 275)

People with commitment! Be dutiful to God and give up the remaining demand against (the loan), if you are indeed people of commitment. Now, if you do not do it, get the signal of war from God and His Ambassador; and if you pledge no repetition, you are entitled to your capital sums. (Make it sure) you neither offend (anyone) nor be offended (by anyone). (2:278–279)

#### Hoarding of Wealth

Persons of Commitment! The truth is that the majority of the priests and ascetics wrongfully devour the wealth of the people and misdirect them away from the way of God. Moreover, there are persons who hoard gold and silver and do not spend them in the way of God. So, inform them about a painful punishment as their fate. It will be on a Day when this (hoarded wealth) will be soldered in the fire of Hell and will be used to scorch their foreheads, flanks and backs: This is what you hoarded for yourselves; now have a taste of what you hoarded. (9: 34–35)

#### Wrongful use of property

Moreover, do not consume each other’s wealth in an unjustified manner, nor use it to bribe the judges so that you may consume some part of the wealth of other people in a sinful way; and that too knowingly. (2:188)

### **V)** **Forbidden degrees in marriage**  

#### **Unbelieving women**

Do not marry with the women with pantheistic beliefs until they commit themselves (to God). A slave woman who is committed (to God) is better than a woman with pantheistic beliefs, even though she may charm you. And do not marry to men with pantheistic beliefs until they commit themselves (to God). A slave man who is committed (to God) is better than a man with pantheistic beliefs, even though he may attract you. They invite you to the Fire; and God invites you to the Garden (of Paradise) and Amnesty through His Signal; and clarifies His Arguments to the mankind; so that they keep admiring Him. (2: 221)

#### Already married women

(Prohibited for you) also are women already married with the exception of women who are your slaves. (4: 24)

#### Wives of fathers

Moreover, do not marry the women your fathers ever married, except what has already happened in the past; it was shameful and foul — truly an obnoxious practice. (4: 22)

#### Near Relatives

Prohibited to you (for marriage) are: your mothers, daughters, sisters; father’s sisters, mother’s sisters; brother’s daughters, sister’s daughters; foster-mothers (who breastfed you), foster-sisters; your wives’ mothers; your step-daughters under your guardianship, born of your wives with whom you have already had intercourse,- there is no prohibition though if you have not yet had intercourse;- the wives of your biological sons ; and two sisters simultaneously in marriage, except for what has already happened in the past…(4: 23)

### **VI)** **Sexual corruption**  

#### **Adultery and Promiscuity**

Moreover, do not ever come close to sex outside marriage; it is undoubtedly an illicit and obnoxious practice. 17: 32)

#### Homosexuality

Moreover, if any two men among you behave illicitly, cause distress to them both! If then they pledge never to repeat the act, and reform themselves, leave them. Certainly God is Kind-to-repentance-Merciful. (4: 16)

#### Forced marriage and Prostitution

People Committed (to System)! It is not approved for you to take hold of women in an undesirable manner. (4: 19)

Moreover, do not force girls under your (protection) to prostitution, despite their desire of decency, with the aim of acquiring some worldly benefits. And in case any ones have been forced into it, God is surely Forgiving-Merciful to such victimised women. (24: 33)

### **VII)** **Murder**  

Moreover, do not take life, which God has made sacrosanct, except for a mandated purpose. (17: 33)

Never should a Devotee (of Islam) kill another Devotee, except if it happens by mistake; and if anyone has committed a murder by mistake, he has to release a slave Devotee and give an adequate compensation to the family of the deceased. (4: 92)

### **VIII)** **Spreading chaos in society**  

Do not foment turmoil on the earth after its reform; and implore him in a state of awe and with intensity of longing! Certainly the Grant of God is close to the people with good conduct. (7: 56)

The reason for contempt is only against the ones who oppress the people and tyrannise the earth without any mandate. A terrible punishment is in store for such (oppressors). (42: 42)

Moreover, guard yourselves against a turmoil the destruction of which would (be generalised and) not limited to the offenders. (8: 25)

People Committed (to Islam)! When you have secret meetings, do not do it for a sinful purpose or for enmity or for an act of disobedience to the Ambassador; and meet one another for good purposes and conscientious dutifulness; and always remain dutiful to God to whom you will all be herded. (58: 9)

There are certain private meetings inspired by Satan, which are aimed at bringing distress to the people committed (to System). (58: 10)
