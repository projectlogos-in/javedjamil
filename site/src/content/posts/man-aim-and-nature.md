#### A Systematic Study of the Holy Qur’an — Part II: Philosophy

> Each man shall reap the fruits of his own deeds: no soul shall bear another’s burden. In the end you shall all return to your Lord, and He will inform you about that in which you differed. (6: 164/Z)

![](https://javedjamilhome.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/09/24979-1aie771ebadv_sgbxep8ebg.jpeg?w=736)  

In Islam, man occupies the central position in universal system. Man is the supreme creation of God; he **is His viceroy or deputy on the Earth**. The universe is governed by God; every creation submits to His Will by following the Laws of Nature established by Him. It is only Man and Jinn that have been bestowed upon with a **free will, which though is subject to the will of God**. Having given man a free will, and the intellect and physical capability to use that free will, man has been given the charge of the Earth; **he has been created for and is expected to govern it in accordance with the guidelines of God**. He is advised to work within the framework of rights, duties and prohibitions, elaborated by God through His messengers. Yet, he is free to even transgress those limits. And it is on the basis of how he sticks to the God’s bounds that he will be tried. Man is therefore **accountable for his activities**; if he follows God’s injunctions, supreme bliss awaits him when he returns to Him; if he violates them or wanders aimlessly, the eternal hell will be his destination.

If it has been said that God recorded everything in the Hidden Tablet before the creation of the universe, **it does not mean that Destiny governs every human act.** Had it been so, man would not have been responsible for his acts. **Recording of the events by God only means that His Knowledge is absolute; He does not depend on the event for knowing** it; He knows everything that will ever occur in the universe. While it is true that nothing can happen without God letting it happen, letting happen implies only permission, not approval. When man commits an error, it is not because God approves or commands it; but because he has let him do it; for he has been given free will and intelligence to decide what he wants to do.

**The aim of the creation of man is his total submission to God. _Abd_ (worship or service) is the word which has been used in Quran only for men and jinns. _Hamd, Sajdah, tasbih and taqdees_ are the words used for other creatures as well. The whole universe as a whole and its creatures other than men and jinn submit to the commands of God as their _fitrah_ (inherent nature). _Abd_ on the other hand is conscious decision to submit; if they want they can even refuse to accept Him. _Abd_ is therefore the highest state of submission and means worship involving conscious devotion and love for God. As he is capable of rising to the highest state of submission, man is the supreme creature of God who has been given intellect and free will. It is now up to him to prove to be a true worshipper and servant or a rebel of God.**

> In the name of God, the Kind, the Merciful

### MAN IN THE QUR’AN

#### Man has aim: Viceroy on Earth

Does man calculate that he will be left aimless? (75: 36)

Listen! Your Lord said to the Executives (Angels): “I am about to appoint a viceroy on the earth.” (2: 30)

Moreover, it is He who has made you (human beings) the caliphs of the Earth; and has given you relative superiority over one another in ranks (in diverse areas) so that He may examine you all in accordance with what He has granted to you. Of course, your Lord is prompt in (declaring) the Results; and surely, He is Pardoning-Merciful. (6: 165)

#### Purpose of creation of man

I did not create Jinn and Man for anything else but exclusively devoting to Me. (61: 56)

Next: We are going to test you with several kinds of terror and hunger, loss of wealth, lives or the produce; but announce to the People of Perseverance — when a calamity afflicts them, they say: “To God We relate, and toward Him We move” — that they are going to be bestowed upon with blessings and favours from their Lord, and they are the recipients of perpetual guidance. (2: 155–157)

You will be examined in matters related to your wealth and your personal affairs; moreover, you will be subjected to a lot of verbal onslaughts at the hands of the recipients of previous scriptures and pantheists; subsequently if you respond with perseverance and remain conscientiously on your guard, this will be a proof of your firm determination. (3: 186)

#### Mankind wrongs itself

Of course, God does not do injustice to mankind in any way whatsoever; on the contrary, mankind does injustice to itself. (10: 44)

#### Each man reaps fruits

Every person is to get the return only in accordance with what is his responsibility; none is to carry the burden of anyone else. Following that, you all are destined to move back to your Lord; then He will settle the issues of dispute among you. (6: 164)

#### Man Thankless

Further, it was We Who settled you on the (Planet) Earth and made for you means of sustenance in it; you are misers in thankfulness. (7: 10/)

Children of Adam, do not let Satan create trouble for you the way he got your parents banished from the Garden, stripping them so that their privacy got exposed on them; certainly he and his tribe watch you from a position where you cannot see them; certainly We have made Satans patrons of the persons who do not commit (to God). (7: 27)

#### Will of God supreme

Further, it is not possible for the soul to commit (to God) without the signal of God; and He makes (the souls) of the persons with no intelligence unclean. (10: 100)

This is no less than a Memorandum for the Metaverse; for anyone who is determined to tread the Established Track; and your act of determining is subject to determining by God the Lord of the Metaverse. (81: 27–29)

….He helps (in emerging victorious) whoever He determines; and He is Mighty-Merciful.(30: 5)

#### Man the best creation

Mankind, what has puffed you with arrogance regarding your Bounteous Lord, the One who (first) created you, then properly proportioned you, and gave you all a well-judged social presence in whatever form He determined? Oh no! In sharp contrast, you act against the System (of God)(82: 6–9)

#### Man’s deeds recorded

No problem can afflict the earth or your persons without its being already there in the Book (of Events) before it actually happens. Of course, it is easy for God; it means that you should not feel unduly sad about losses, and should not be unduly jubilant over your gains; and (remember that) God does not love boastful, arrogant persons. They are the people who are greedy and advocate greediness in human society. Moreover, anyone who deviates (should know) that God is Rich-Glorified. (57: 22–24)

Further, We have inserted every person’s fate-sheet in his neck; and We will bring it out delivering as an open readable document to them. “Read your document, your own self is sufficient enough to demonstrate your result to you on the Day.” (17: 13–14)

If anyone gets guided, he does so for his own self; and if anyone remains unguided its responsibility too is on himself; none bears the burden of anyone else. Further We do not punish till We produce an ambassador. (17:15)

(Future will witness) the testimony of extremely fast vehicles with horns blowing: they work with engines, then they renew its (fuel) in the morning; then they cause drenching (pollution of air) due to it; then these (pollutants) keep collecting (in atmosphere). Certainly, mankind is passive toward its Lord; and (this is despite the fact) that it witnesses everything itself; and surely it is still extremely fond of luxury. Does it not know that a time will come when the residents of graves will be brought to life again and the contents of their bosoms will be disclosed; surely their Lord will take cognizance of everything that day. (100: 1–11)

#### Remembers God when in distress

Further, when a problem affects a man he keeps imploring Us — lying on his side, sitting or standing. Then when We remove his problem he starts behaving as if he did never implore Us for his problem. How come the misdeeds become so attractive for the transgressors! (10: 12)

#### Sometimes commits blasphemy in despair

If We give Man a taste of favour from Our side, and later withdraws it from him, he often tends to indulge in disobedience (of God). Moreover, if We give him a taste of our grant after a problem has affected him, he tends to say that “the bad times have departed from me” and he celebrates it boastfully, with the exception of some who behave patiently and engage in good deeds; for them there is grant of pardon and a big reward. (11: 9–11)

You cannot simply have a good grant except that it is from God; after this, if a problem affects you, you implore Him with loud cries. Following that, if he removes your problem some groups among you start worshipping gods other than their Lord showing disregard to Our grants. So enjoy! You will soon know (the truth). (16: 53–55/A)

When a wave of the sea shrouds them like a tent they start praying to God and their system (of worship) becomes one of exclusive devotion (to Him); then when we rescue them to the land, some of them dilute (their exclusivity); none deviates from Our guidelines except one who proves to be wholly treacherous and disobedient. (31: 32)

Now, there is also a (type of) persons who on being first tested and then being granted respect and positions by his Lord (boastfully) says “My Lord has given me respect.” And when he is put to tough test through restriction of resources, he says (complaining) “My Lord has insulted me.” (89: 15–16/A)

#### Only good from God

Whatever nice happens to you, it is (a grant) from God; and whatever bad happens to you is on account of yourselves; and We have dispatched an Ambassador for the mankind; and God is enough as witness. (4: 79)

Moreover, whatever problem affects you, you have earned it through your own hands, and (the truth is) that (God) pardons most of (your misdeeds). Moreover, you cannot dominate the earth, and cannot find anyone as your patron apart from God. (42: 30–31)

_\*”Yourselves” in the above means the mankind as a whole rather than individuals. If an individual suffers, it is only because of either his own mistake or the mistake of some of his parents, forefathers, relatives or other human beings._

#### Relief follows distress

Did We not make your bosom receptive to (knowledge and wisdom)? Further, did We not remove your burden, which had stressed your back? Still more, We gave (unparalleled) heights to your renown. (It means that) relief accompanies distress indeed; of course, relief accompanies distress. Now when you have finished your mission, you declare it (over) and attend wholly to (the meditation of) your Lord! (94: 1–8)

#### Man hasty

Moreover, man wishes as much for harmful things as for good ones; as man is always in a hurry. (17: 11)

#### Man impatient

The human being was indeed created impatient (by nature). When something bad affects him he tends to become hopeless; and when some gains embrace him, he tends to be mean. (70: 19–21)

The human being was created with urgency in nature. You will view My clues shortly; hence, better not show urgency. (21: 37)

I swear by this city! You are a citizen of this city by right. By father and the child! We have created human being in a state of perpetual struggle. Does he calculate that he will never be conquered by anyone else? He (boastfully) says, “I have squandered a lot of wealth.” Does he calculate that nobody has kept a watch on him? Have We not formed for (humans) two eyes to see, and a tongue and two lips? We have also shown him two courses. Still, he has failed to tread the path of Progress. And (We wish) he knew what Progress is. (It implies) the freedom of slave, giving (someone) food in his time of hunger; to an orphan among relatives or to a needy person in times of misery. In addition to them he must (of course) be among those who have committed themselves (to God), have exhorted one another for maintaining resolve with perseverance and have exhorted one another to be compassionate (in behaviour). (90:1–17)

#### Man greedy

Moreover, you consume the produce all alone; and your love for wealth dominates over every other (love). (89: 19–20)

Mutual competition distracts you (from the Path) till you reach the graves. No way! You are surely going to know (the reality). (102: 1–3)

State: Everyone acts in accordance with his own designs; your Lord knows who is right on the Track. (17: 84)

#### Good Conduct is the Key to Success

(There stands the) testimony of the night when it shrouds everything (in darkness), the day when it becomes bright and the One who created male and female (sexes) that your efforts are obviously diverse (in aims and methods). So the one who pays (his dues), acts conscientiously and works with supreme honesty will be taken by Us to a state of supreme relaxation. And the one who acts miserly and regards himself too rich (to think of anything else) and regards the excellence (in deeds) futile, will be taken by Us to a state of utter distress, and when he is thrown in it, his wealth will be of no avail. (92: 1–11)

Your riches and children are more often than not sources of trouble; and (in store) with God is a great reward. (64: 15)

Time is testimony to the reality that mankind is in a state of loss except the persons who are committed (to Islam), engage in good deeds and campaign for (implementing the) the Mandate and for constantperseverance. (103: 1–3)

There is testimony of Fig, Olive and Mount Sinai and this City of Trust (all related to covenants between God and people) that We have created mankind in the best scheme of collective existence. Later We totally dismissed it (from Our favour) with the exception of the persons who commit themselves (to Islam) and engage in good deeds. For them in store is an unfailing reward. (95: 1–6/)

_\*(Note: Fig, Olive and Mount Sinai signify the position regarding covenants between God and Jews which they later broke. Jesus, as many Christian authorities argued, cursed Fig because it symbolized Jews by not giving the expected fruits. Olive was considered a symbol of peace in Old Testament and Mount Sinai represented the Testament between Moses and His people. In Makkah too Prophet took a promise from the people at the time of his last sermon on the occasion of Hajj.)_

Were they created from nothing? Or were they their own creators? (52: 35)
