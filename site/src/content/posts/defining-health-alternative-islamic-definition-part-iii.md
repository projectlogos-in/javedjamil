#### **Quran’s Position**

Islam on the other hand suggests a much more comprehensive definition of health. Let us examine the following verses of the Holy Qur’an, related to _Health, Reproductive & Child Health and Family Health:_

#### **Hygienic food**

_State: Who prohibits you from availing the attractions and the wholesome items of consumption produced by God for His subjects? (7: 32)_

#### **Environment**

_Do not foment turmoil on the earth after its reform; and implore him in a state of awe-inspired devotion and with intensity of longing! Certainly the Grant of God is close to the people with good conduct. (7: 56)_

_And there is the sky which He extended to great heights establishing equilibrium (in it) so that you may not disturb the equilibrium. (55: 7–8)_

#### **Personal Hygiene**

_(It is) in a perfectly guarded Testament, which no one can touch except the perfectly clean. (56: 78–79)_

_And keep your garments clean. (74: 4)_

#### **Unhealthy food prohibited**

_Items prohibited for you are: the meat of the dead (animals), blood, the pork, the meat which has been dedicated to a deity other than God, the flesh of the animals killed by strangulation, or by a violent blow, or by a fall on the head side, or by being gored, or left after hunting by a wild animal, unless you are able to properly slaughter it, or (that of an animal) sacrificed at altars. Further, taking decisions through divining arrows is a gross violation (of the code). (5: 3)_

#### **Unhealthy social practices prohibited**

_They ask you about the substances of addiction and gambling. State: “There is huge sin and some benefits for the mankind in them; but the sin is much greater than their benefits.” (2: 219)_

#### **Mental and spiritual health**

_In the wake of this, there will be some from among them who would commit themselves (to Islam), promote perseverance and promote welfare activities. (90: 17)_

_People committed (to Islam)! Be perseverance and constant; coordinate (with others) and remain dutiful to God so that you become successful. (3: 200)_

_They are the persons who persevere with patience and have (undiluted) trust in God. (16: 42)_

#### **_Sexual Hygiene_**

_People committed (to Islam)! Do not come close to offering Salaat (Reading Blessings) with your mind intoxicated, till you are in a position to understand what you utter, nor in a state of impurity caused by ejaculations, except when you are travelling in the way, till you have taken bath. (4: 43)_

_Next; they ask you regarding women’s periods. State: They are like an injury; so keep distance from women in their courses and do not have physical proximity with them till they become clean; once they have become clean, you may go to them within the boundaries God has enacted for you. Certainly God loves the Seekers of Repentance as well as the Seekers of Purity. (2: 222)_

_State: My Lord has prohibited nothing but the illicit relations, open as well as secret, the (known) sins and the unauthenticated violations. (7: 33)_

_Moreover, do not ever come close to sex outside marriage; it is undoubtedly an illicit behaviour and an obnoxious practice. (17: 32)_

#### **Homosexuality forbidden**

_Moreover, if any two men among you behave illicitly, punish both of them! If then they pledge never to repeat the act, and reform themselves, leave them. Certainly God is Kind-to-repentance-Merciful. (4: 16)_

#### **Mother and Child Health**

_For those who are burdened (with pregnancy), their period is till they get free of their burden. (65: 4)_

_Moreover, it is not right for them to conceal what God has created in their wombs, if they are committed to God and the Last Day; and in that period their husbands have the right to annul the divorce, if they decide to reconcile. (2: 228)_

_Moreover, if they happen to be pregnant, spend on them till they get completely free of their burden. Following that, if they breastfeed the (children), (it is your duty to) give them an adequate remuneration through a mutual consultation in a respectable way. If you have any disputes, let the (child) be breastfed by someone else. (65: 6)_

_The mothers should breastfeed their children for two whole years; this direction is for anyone who desires to complete the term of breastfeeding. And he, the father, is (directed) to bear the cost of their food and clothing in an appreciable manner. (However,) nobody is burdened with more than one can bear. Neither the mother has to be harmed on account of her child nor the father on account of his child; similar is the case of an heir. If they both decide on weaning their child, by mutual consent, there is nothing wrong in it on their part. If you choose to get your child breastfed by some other woman, it is no crime on your part either, provided you pay (the woman) what you offered in an appreciable manner. And remain dutiful to God knowing that God watches all your actions. (2: 233)_

#### **Prophet Muhammad (PBUH) and Lifestyle**

Apart from the major directions that Qur’an gives regarding the role of health in the establishment of the system of governance, Prophet Muhammad’s deeds and sayings also give general guidelines related to lifestyle including eating habits and exercises. Here it is to be stated, especially for the benefit of those who are not aware about the Islamic System, Muhammad (PBUH) was the Last Messenger of God, and the recipient of the Last Testament of God known as Qur’an. His deeds and expressions are known as Sunnah and Hadith are regarded as the elaboration, explanation and interpretation of Qur’an, and he is the ultimate Model to imitate in Islamic scheme of things. This particular work is mainly about presenting an alternative Islamic paradigm of health and not about the detailed commentary on the health benefits of the Prophetic choices, which have received considerable attention and a significant amount of literature is already available. However, a few things need to be mentioned here.

**The first and foremost is to assert that unlike the modern concept of “freedom of choice”, Prophet’s were always good choices. The modern concept is the brainchild of the forces of economics that seek to commercialize every choice and does not make a distinction between good and bad choices. God, His Final Testament and His Messenger (PBUH), as has already been discussed, give only the choices that are acceptable from the point of view of comprehensive peace, which includes health as its most important component.**

Let us first talk about the Prophet’s choices in matters of nutrition. Here it needs to be stressed that **contrary to the popular perception, Prophet did not stress a non-vegetarian regime. Instead the emphasis was on a balanced mixture of all the good nutrients — meat, milk, fruits, vegetables and other plant products including seeds and herbs.** In short, both animal and plant products are of equal importance and are to be taken in optimum quantities. Animal products including flesh and milk products are rich source of essential amino acids, iron and many vitamins. Plant products are rich source of fibre, vitamins and minerals. **The Prophet however did utter words that showed some disliking about the red meat, particularly the meat of cow. A Hadith says: _“From Mulaikah bint ‘Amr: Prophet (PBUH) said: “The milk of the cattle contains healing, its fat is a medicine, and its meat (causes) sickness.” (Tabarani)”_ It may either specifically refer to cow meat (_T. saginata_, a parasite present in cow meat can cause some health problems, which though are not as severe as caused by _Taenia solium_ and _Trichinella spiralis_ present in pork) or the beef in general, which tends to increase the fats and cholesterol level in body. Qur’an talks of birds’ meat as the one, which will be among the delicacies of Paradise. Fruits of various kinds of course, similar as well as different from what are available on the earth, will be in abundant supply.** The Prophet was very critical of overeating with several sayings about the need to eat just short of a full appetite. He advised to drink a lot of water. Prophet Muhammad preferred barley floor, loved honey, vinegar, olive oil, dates, black seeds, etc. All these have not only great nutritious values as the suppliers of energy, vitamins and minerals but have also been shown to have beneficial effects in various diseases. There is a need for further research in this area, as unfortunately the modern allopathic system of medicine has not taken adequate interest in this subject and has left it for the experts of alternative medicine to talk about them.

The Messenger of God also warned against keeping eatables uncovered and followed a strict personal regime of total hygiene including washing of private organs after each act of urination and intercourse, washing of hands before eating and proper rinsing of mouth after eating. Of course, as previously described he gave huge emphasis on brushing the teeth with Miswak.

Prophet Muhammad (PBUH) loved physical exercises and athletics. He also stressed a good sleep speaking against praying the whole night. He preferred to go early to bed and rise early in the morning.

**Evidently, all his preferences and dislikes were in accordance with their impact on health. He made an important policy statement, _“There is no disease that Allah has created without creating its treatment”_ (Al-Bukhari). There are verses and Hadiths describing the well-being as the greatest or one of the greatest gifts of God. In another statement Prophet said, _“Allah has never created a disease but created its treatment, which is known to some people and unknown to others, except death. (At-Tabarani)”_ Abu Dawud quotes him saying that _“Allah has sent down both the disease and the cure, and He has appointed a cure for every disease, so treat yourselves medically, but use nothing unlawful.”_ Another Hadith indicated that except old age all diseases have treatments even if we do not know them.**

On the basis of the above quoted verses and the positions taken by Prophet Muhammad (PBUH), it is not difficult to conclude that:

First, in Islam, **health is not a separate entity but one of the essential constituents of peace**, which comprise peace at the individual, family and social level. (It in fact includes peace in this life and Hereafter.) In the current world scenario on the other hand, peace means only an absence of wars and civil wars, and the international organisations define peace and health in a way that they seem to be totally different entities.

Second, in Islam, **there are three tiers of society: individual, family and society. All three have equal importance and none can be sacrificed through the sword of the other. Health therefore too has to involve all the three.** The three have a mutual relationship that makes them inseparable. Individuals have to establish a disciplined family and social system. In return, family and society have to protect individuals from all kinds of external threats. The current international system ignores family system and aims to cater only to the individual.

Third, Islam classifies acts into following categories:

1.  _Halal_ (Permissible), without being obligatory or desirable;
2.  _Fardh_ (obligatory);
3.  _Mustahab_ (Desirable), without being obligatory;
4.  _Haram_ (Prohibited):
5.  _Makruh_ (undesirable) without prohibited.

This categorisation is extremely important. This in fact makes Islam the most scientific system of the world. It also gives certain flexibility to the constitution that makes life easy for even those having weaker convictions.

The Holy Qur’an itself declares that it allows what is good and forbids what is bad. Now, after studying various prohibitions and obligations in Islam on the basis of the till now available scientific knowledge, we find the categorisation of acts, where they are related with health, to be based on the following principles:

1.  **Islam declares prohibited all those things and practices that do not form parts of the normal requirements of the body and can directly lead to the development of a disease, which more often than not becomes fatal or causes physical handicaps like blindness, paralysis, etc. (Alcohol, pork, unhealthy sexual practices)**
2.  **Islam declares _makruh_ or undesirable all those things and practices, which may harm the health but are neither usually fatal, nor lead to any physical handicap like blindness, paralysis etc.**
3.  **Islam declares _Mustahab_ or desirable or _Fardh_ (obligatory) all those things and practices that have beneficial effect on the health of the individual or society in general (Circumcision, several foods, Ablution, Prayer, _Istanja_, etc.)**

In the light of these observations, we can now give **an Islamic definition of health**, which is as follows:

> **_“Health is a state of complete physical, mental, spiritual and social well being, which must be safeguarded not only through the maintenance of a health preserving regime at the personal/individual level, but also through the establishment of a health-protective and promoting family system and a health-protective and promoting social system.”_**

We shall see how this definition would give sleepless nights to all the proponents of globalisation and their stooges in the World Health Organisation and other health related international bodies and agencies. **The WHO has never insisted on identifying practices that are not suitable for humans and must therefore be totally prohibited, legally and socially, through effective measures at the national and international level**. Whenever campaigns have emerged and intensified against certain practices like smoking, the suggestions have been not to impose total ban on these practices and the commercialisation of harmful substances and practices but only to create awareness among the people about the adverse effects of their actions. The same approach is visible when we study the LaLonde report. The report suggested that there are four general determinants of health, which he called “human biology”, “environment”, “lifestyle”, and “healthcare organisation”. So, _“health is maintained through the science and practice of medicine but can also be improved by individual effort. Physical fitness, weight loss, a healthy diet, stress management, training and stopping smoking and other substances are examples of steps to improve one’s health. Workplace programs are recognised by an increasingly large number of companies for their value in improving health and well-being of their employees, and increasing morale, loyalty and productivity at work. A Company may provide a gym with exercise equipment, start smoking cessation programs, provide nutrition, weight or stress management training. Other programmes may include health risk assessments, health screenings and body mass index monitoring.”_ **The report obviously stresses only the awareness and training programmes for individuals without even being lightly suggestive of any legal and executive measures to ensure a health protective family and society, where individuals are not exposed to dangerous substances and practices.** What to speak of a health protective society, the international system is allowing society to become provocative for the practices that are dangerous from medical point of view.

**Another glaring weakness of the WHO definition is that it fails to properly explain the “spiritual” component of health.** Some researchers tend to describe spiritual health as a feeling of happiness and contentment. Islam will help us to better understand the difference between “mental health” and “spiritual health”. Mental health means the ability to respond normally or adequately to the various situations, to possess a mental balance, a state of mental calm without anxiety or fear and not to have any palpable disorders of thinking. When we say a patient is “mentally ill”, it means that the person is having problems of sleep, anxiety, fear, concentration, etc or his thinking process has become incongruous. He has become either “crazy” (Neurotic) or “mad” (Psychotic). Merriam-Webster defines mental health as _“a state of emotional and psychological well-being in which an individual is able to use his or her cognitive and emotional capabilities, function in society, and meet the ordinary demands of everyday life.”_ Spiritual health, on the contrary, means an individual’s ability to distinguish between right and wrong and resolve to lead a more righteous life, free of stratagems, deception, dishonesty and selfishness.
