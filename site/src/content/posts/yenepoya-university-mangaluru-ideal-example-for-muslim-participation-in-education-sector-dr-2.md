#### Report by A Yaqub

![](https://javedjamilhome.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/09/527e9-1xy5nvghpzefqdaozuo02wa2x.jpeg?w=736)  

Internationally renowned thinker and writer and Chair in Islamic Studies, Yenepoya University has described “Yenepoya University, Mangaluru as an ideal example of Muslim participation in Education Sector in India”. He was delivering Keynote Address in Muslim Empowerment programme organised jointly by IDB, Muslim Educational Front and Farooqya Dental College, Mysuru at Mysuru. Dr. Jamil said that mere movement for education among Muslims is not enough and Muslim entrepreneurs and organisations must also look for large-scale participation in Educational industry. He said that “the founders of Yenepoya University have given us a shining example of a highly successful educational venture.” He called upon the educationists of the country to take a cue from the highly professional approach of the Managers of Yenepoya University, Mr Abdullah Kunhi and Mr. Farhad Yenepoya “Whose efforts have put the University not only in Grade A category according to NAC but also in the 100-best-university club.”

![](https://javedjamilhome.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/09/58524-1ioqunohvhec17j70qgq8ea2x.jpeg?w=736)  

Dr. Jamil argued that if Muslims have to empower themselves, they have to work for a comprehensive plan of empowerment comprising Ideological, Economic, Social and Political Empowerment. He said that Muslims should know that they are not just a minority, but also ideologically second largest majority and socially part of the deprived majority. He called upon Muslims not just to work for their own community but for the whole nation, especially the deprived. He said that Economic Inequality and rising crime rates and social vices like Alcohol and Commercialisation of Sex and Nudity are the biggest problems of the country and Muslims must take all the communities together in the fight to build a healthy, peaceful and prosperous nation.

Prof. Akhil Ahmad said that sincerity and comprehensive planning are the mainstays of development and knowledge is the biggest tool in this process. He argued that Quran provided the ideal principles for the process of development. Dr Mubarak Kapadi argued that social reforms are the key to prosperity and if the families are not peaceful, children cannot be expected to achieve big results. The inaugural session was also addressed Prof. Shaikh Ali and Mr. Taj Mohhammad Khan.
