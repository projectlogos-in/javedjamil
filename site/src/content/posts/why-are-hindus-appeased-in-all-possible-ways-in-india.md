#### Frank Speak to Indian Hindu Brothers: Dr. Javed Jamil’s Reply to D C Nath — 5

In the Name of God, Kind-Merciful, the Only One worthy of Worship, every other entity, animate or inanimate, great or holy, being His humble servant

#### To continue

#### DC Nath

![](https://javedjamilhome.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/09/33f79-0yra-khxf5rfdmtam.jpg?w=736)  

> As for our affinity or otherwise with the NDA government, we fondly believe to be working as the “Ombudsman”. That is, we do point out their fault lines, and do it rather strongly when warranted.

> We have, in this context, some questions to our Muslim brothers in general that we are putting forth here to you. Before that, let us note some facts:  
>    
> a) That some months back, the Minister of State for Minority Affairs had gone on “Bharat Darshan” tour to assure the Muslim fraternity that they ought to have no apprehension from the Modi Sarkar. We strongly felt that was totally unwarranted. And, we have told all concerned about that.

#### Javed Jamil

First the “Ombudsman” seems to have examined the affairs of only Minority Ministry and the statements of “appeasement” of minorities. It did not show any interest in how the rapid privatisation and doling of everything to certain groups of corporates is bound to hit the economy in the long run, how much it is going to further enhance the Economic Disparity and how much damage the communal card of the Modi’s party is going to cause to the nation. It also failed in its duty as “ombudsman” to have a frank analysis of how much Modi Sarkar has succeeded in keeping its promises.

As regards Bharat Darshan, if a Modi Minister undertook it, it was simply a failed effort to wash its long record of BJP’s anti- Muslim campaign, and to try out to wipe out Modi’s image of the “Butcher of Gujarat”. The interesting fact is that while you are so much aware of her “Bharat Darshan” to the pint of being extremely annoyed, I remain totally unaware of it. It could have been a welcome exercise had it been done in sincerity. Muslims would have even forgiven the long list of sins and crimes against them. But if there was any such exercise, this exercise was nothing more than eyewash. Recent events have proved that the BJP and its leaders and sister organisations are busy in creating a kind of communal frenzy, which would break into big riots at some part of time, the time obviously depending upon the choice of BJP. 2017 may turn out to be the preferred time if my calculations are right. Mr Modi has great past experience of how riots can sustain power for long. With its failure on socioeconomic front, Modi Srakar would be left with no option but to instigate riots and hope that like Gujarat, one big riot, will ensure power for Modi for at least next ten years. But their political calculations may wrong, as India is not Gujarat.

Your concern should have been rather otherwise, that the government has totally failed to earn the confidence of minorities, which are feeling much more insecure than before. Unfortunately, this seems to be the aim of the Hindutva lobby, to make Muslims feel insecure and intimidated. They forget that such widespread alienation and feeling of insecurity almost always boomerangs, and weak and insecure try to find their own ways to vent their frustration.

By acting as “Ombudsman” of only Minority Affairs Ministry you have proved that you want to ensure that nothing substantial is done to help Muslims recover from its huge backwardness. I am sorry top say but from what you have written makes one feel that you and your colleagues in the Hindutva Brigade seem to derive pleasure in the alienation, backwardness, helplessness and fear of Muslims.

#### DC Nath

![](https://javedjamilhome.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/09/46f2b-0u-_xryuqwyzxit8q.jpg?w=736)  

> b) Then, it was the turn of the Minister for Minority Affairs herself (Nazma Heptullah) to take this forward. We protested against that also.

#### Javed Jamil

It means you do not want any single effort to alleviate the sufferings of Muslims, even if they are more cosmetic than real. We also dismiss such cosmetic exercises as Minority Ministry seems to be taking. I have yet to notice any worthwhile efforts to improve the conditions of Muslims, the way there have been for poor and Dalit and OBC Hindus. Even the Budget for the Minority Ministry has gone down.

#### DC Nath

> c) After that, comes the Vice President Hamid Ansari. During his interaction with the delegation from the All India Majlish-e-Mushawarat, he empathised in no uncertain terms with the fate of the Muslims as to how much remained towards ameliorating their conditions of living. We demanded that if what we had said basing on a report from the “Indian Express” was incorrect, let a note on denial be issued from his office. That has not happened.

#### Javed Jamil

How silly! The Vice President of the country cannot raise the issues related to the citizens of the country! He has every right to show his concern on any issue. He has in fact performed his duty in raising concerns about the fate of the Muslim community in the country. And the alienation, insecurity and backwardness of a big community, which forms 15 pc of the population, are certainly big issues demanding attention of everyone, from President of the country to the lowest officers and politicians. This again proves that you want to keep Muslims backward, and hate anyone raising concern about their pathetic conditions. You simply want all benefits to be distributed among Hindus, and those too, mainly among the Upper Caste Hindus. This simply proves that you and your friends have absolutely no love or sympathy for fellow Muslim Indians.

#### DC Nath

> d) Now, at the level of the government. On October 5, 2015, there had been a full-page ad by the Ministry Of Minority Affairs in all major national dailies (may be in vernacular dailies also) on “Jashan-e-Ustad,subtitled: “India’s Creative Continuum — Minority Communities In Craft”. As a learned scholar has beautifully observed, “This is another instance of minority (Muslim) appeasement policy. Thus, it has become an official policy to distinguish between artisans on the basis of their religion (at the cost of quality) and thus foster this difference”.

#### Javed Jamil

For you, doling reservation to 50 pc Hindus is no appeasement of Hindus, making all schemes that benefit upper caste Hindu corporates is no appeasement of Hindus, celebrating Yoga Day as a national event is no appeasement of Hindus, making special arrangements for Hindu pilgrims, giving almost all the party tickets to Hindus, giving daily statements to please Hindus and harass Muslims, changing educational policies to inculcate what they call Hindu cum Indian culture, giving only copies of Gita to foreign Presidents — all these are not Hindu Appeasement. If the Government plans Smart Cities, Small Villages, FDI retail moves, even Bank and Insurance schemes — all these percolate to benefit the corporates, almost all of whom are the upper caste Hindus.

Yes, you are right, ideally it should not be called Appeasement; it is in fact total surrender and total apportioning of all benefits to Hindus. And you are right, few doling of cosmetic benefits to Muslims can only be called political appeasement and no substantive assistance. Let BJP declare that they will not do absolutely anything for solving the problems of Muslims. This would be more honest for them. Ironically, when other parties try to do something for Muslims, all these being nothing more than cosmetic exercises, BJP calls it appeasement. When it does something itself, it claims: Look how concerned we are for Muslims!

I would like to quote here excepts from a letter to me by Mr Abdul Rashid, who has been associated with Mishra Commission, about his feelings on your charge against the Ministry of Minority Affairs:

“One fails to understand why Mr Nath is so angry in extending certain welfare schemes for minorities (not Muslims). Being such a senior official, he should understand that Min. Of Minority Affairs was carved out of the then Min. Of Social Justice and Empowerment and most of the schemes were already there for OBCs which included Muslims also. Only scholarship schemes have been slightly improved but with the income rider that even a class IV Muslim employee cannot avail it. Mr Nath should rather be happy that the benefits are being extended to poor and OBC Muslims 90% of whom are converts. Does he have no regards for former chief Justice Misra. Before favouring reservations on economic criteria, let Mr Nath study Indian constitution which favours such concessions to socially and educationally backward classes only. Muslims need no favour but only equal treatment and opportunity. Why numerous benefits are being extended to SCs and STs only because they are Hindus and Sikhs and Budhs only. Why discriminatory Article 341 continues to exist in the statute?

“As regards continuation of the welfare schemes by Modi govt ,Najma is trying her best to dilute them by giving new names. Our efforts to relax rigid conditions for award of scholarship have fallen on deaf years. As regards Burdwan Madarsa, why did NSA chose to visit this place only and not dozens of places like Nanded, Kanpur etc where Sangh Pariwar outfits were involved in bomb blast cases. Why does a scheme like Nai Roshni prick Mr Nath, does he not care for poor women? As regards Naqvi,s tour in selected places, everybody knows it is for capturing votes of Muslims only and nothing more.”

#### DC Nath

> Was this necessary? Was any non-Muslim artisan included/searched for and selected? Why not a similar attempt made to select artisans from the tribal community, some of whom are noted for their innate artistic skills? Where from did the huge amount of money come. It was certainly from the public exchequer, in which all of us pay.

#### Javed Jamil

If tribal community needs it, they should also be covered. But for them there are other departments. And surely they receive much more attention and much more benefits than Muslims. Muslims have nothing more than poor artisans. And if the government tries to take advantage of their talent, it is a positive but very small step. Or do you want, that Muslims should get starved and die? And “Public Exchequer” — Does it not include taxes from Muslims or are they exempted from paying any taxes? Do they not pay Sales Tax on the items they consume? Do they not pay income tax corresponding to their incomes?

#### DC Nath

> Before we go to the questions, a point of ancestry. As it is known, over 90 per cent of Muslim brothers are convertees — some only 3 or 4 generations back, and it has also scientifically been proved that our DNA is the same. Given this background, will not the Indian Muslims lay any claim to country’s rich Indic Civilisation, now known to be over 6000 years old? We are saying this on the basis of the historical truth that rich ancestry is a matter of great pride for every individual. In other words, the great and age-old Indic Civilisation is the legal inheritance of the Indian Muslims? Why then quarrel amongst ourselves?

#### Javed Jamil

There is absolutely no need of quarrel, if Hindus do understand that Muslims are as much Indians as they are, not the Second class citizens; if they realise the immense contribution by Muslims to Indian Civilization and Culture, if they realise that they are their own brothers in need of sympathy, security and support, and if they bury their hatred for them. Even my ancestors were Brahamins. I love all humans, and love Hindus more because they are our immediate neighbours and distant relatives, and share common culture and language.

All these concerns about “Appeasement” of Muslims, and to a lesser extent about the schemes favouring Dalits and OBC Hindus, emanate from the desire to continue the centuries-old domination of Upper Caste Hindus. In the remote past, Kshatriyas ruled with the help of the intellectual power of Brahamins and Money power of Vaishyas. Then Brahamins started ruling the country with the help of the money power of Vaishyas and the Muscle power of Kshatriyas. More recently, Vaishyas have taken the reins in their hands turning democracy into Corporatocracy, with the help of the intellectual power of a section (not all) of Brahamins and the muscle power of Kshatriysa. This is the most dangerous phase because when the money rules, morality soon gets cremated with full honours. But the protagonists of Hindutva should know that Dalits, OBCs and most Muslims not only belong to the same class and have common ancestral roots but also share similar beliefs regarding secularism, justice and equality. Together these classes constitute around 90 pc of India, and you like it or not, it is they along with a large number of those upper caste members, who value secularism, equality and justice, who will play a dominant role in future India and will outsmart the forces of monopolisation.

I am leaving for you the list 0f 100 richest Indians (Forbes List) and leave it for you to count how many of them are from minorities and how many from Hindu castes other than Vaishyas. Please also add their combined wealth. This will give you an idea of just how Vaishya community has already monopolised India’s wealth, and to perpetuate it, they will of course try to use politicians, media and bureaucrats.
