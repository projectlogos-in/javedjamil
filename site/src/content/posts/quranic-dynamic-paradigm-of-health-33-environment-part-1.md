The Holy Qur’an speaks of the universe as a grand State ruled by One Majestic King. This universal system survives due to an extraordinary discipline with every single particle following, without failing, the laws of the nature the Lord established. While the laws of nature control the universe as a whole, the planet Earth has biological laws too in place. In addition, mankind has been given spiritual and social laws to follow. While the laws of nature and Biological laws are beyond human control, social and spiritual laws are largely within the powers of human beings. The earth has a unique system in which mankind plays an extremely important role in the events that take place here.

The Book of God speaks on several occasions on the functioning of the universe and importance of harmony at every level. Qur’an says:

· _And He (God) raised the sky high and set equilibrium in it, so that you may not violate the balance; establish the measure with justness, and don’t harm the equilibrium. (55: 3–13/A)_

· _He who created_

_The seven heavens_

_One above another:_

_No want of proportion_

_Wilt thou see_

_In the Creation_

_Of (God) Most Gracious._

_So turn thy vision again:_

_Do you any flaw?_

_Again turn your vision_

_A second time: your vision_

_Will come back to you_

_Dull and discomfited,_

_In a state worn out. 67: 3–5/A)_

Then the Book gives lengthy descriptions of how God has made the earth a secure place for its inhabitants and how its various constituents help in the survival of the creatures including human beings. See the following verses (- the translation of these verses is by the author himself -):

#### Security

· _The creation of the cosmic tiers along with the earth is a much bigger exercise than the creation of human beings; but the truth is that the majority of mankind does not understand it. (40: 57)_

· _Can’t they see what is ahead of them and what is behind them (in space and time), in the sky and the earth? If We determine, We can cause the earth to swallow them or cause a piece from the sky to fall on them. Certainly there is a strong argument in this for the attentive devotees. (34: 9)_

· _Can’t they see that We get the earth to shrink from all sides? When God orders, none can reverse it; and He is (infinitely) quick in calculation. (13: 41)_

#### _Earth worth inhabiting_

· _Or, Who initiated the creation and following that sustains it; further, who provides to you your requirements from the sky and the earth; is there any other god accompanying God? Challenge them to produce a proof if they claim to be right. (27: 64)_

#### _Rivers, roads and mountains_

· _Or who (if not He) has formed the earth a place suitable for inhabiting, along with rivers passing through it and immovable mountains standing on it…? (27: 61)_

· _Moreover, We have formed mountains in the earth in order to prevent the earth from quaking along with them; and We have formed roads within it for passing through so that they may reach their destinations. (21: 31)_

· _Have We not made the earth a lap like (structure); and the mountains like pegs (78: 6–7)_

_(The earth acts like a lap of the mother or cradle as it provides creatures whatever they need. The mountains are like pegs as they with their heavy weights and their roots going down through the strata of the earth, will help these strata remaining fixed. This can be demonstrated by moving a wooden plate having four or five layers of papers spread over it. If the plate moves, the layers will shake; but if at certain points, very heavy weights are put or pegs are fixed, the papers will remain fixed to the surface of the plate. The fact that earthquakes often lead to the formation of mountains also points to the fact that mountains are natural attempt to counter quakes.)_

### _Water sources:_

#### _Rains_

*   _Further, it is He who rained water from the sky in a measured way and rejuvenated with it the moribund town; you are going to be brought out in the same way. It is He who created pairs of everything and formed ships and animals for you to ride so that you may comfortably set yourselves on their backs, and after you settle there, admire the gift of your Lord saying: “ It is(Your) Force (of Universe) that has subjected them to our use; otherwise we could never have achieved this. (43: 11–13)_
*   _A Sign for them is the moribund land: We rejuvenate it, and bring out grain from it, which you eat; and We grow in it orchards of date-palms and vines, and We make springs gush forth from it to make it possible that they enjoy its products. It was not their hands that accomplished this: will they not be thankful? (36: 33–35)_
*   _It is God who flows different types of air masses; now they vertically lift the clouds; now He horizontally scatters them in the sky the way He determines and forms their condensed droplets; now you observe the raindrops falling from their midst; now he makes them reach His subjects in accordance with His determining; look! They are celebrating. They were of course speechless with hopelessness before (the rain) was made to fall on them. (30: 48–49)_
*   _Don’t you observe that God gently moves the clouds; next He generates attraction between them; next He forms them into small concentrated particles; now you see the rain issuing from their midst. Further, He makes the hail fall from the mountain-like\* clouds in the sky; He strikes any place with them subject to His determining; and He turns them away from any place subject to His determining; the flash of His lightening is almost blinding. It is God who keeps night and day cycling; surely there is a strong lesson in this for the people of comprehensive vision. (24: 43–44) (\*called Cumulonimbus in technical terms)_
*   _Did you not observe how God descended water from the sky and brought out products of different colours with it? There are many streaks of various shades in the mountains, white and red, and of various shades and intense black; similarly there are different colours of men, crawlers and cattle. The learned among His servicemen feel the awe of God; God is Indeed Powerful-Pardoner (35: 27–28)._
*   _And how many an animal there is that do not manufacture their own food; God apportions provision to them as well as for you. He is the Hearer-Prescient. (29: 60)_
*   _We rained water from the sky in right amount and secured it in the earth; and We are capable of removing all of it. With this (water) We grow for you gardens of date-palm and vine; there are plenty of fruits produced there that you take. There is also a particular tree growing on Mount Sinai that produces oil and colour for use as food. Further, there is a grand lesson for you in the cattle, too; you drink the product of their organs; and there are many other benefits in them for you; you carry your goods on them as well as the ships that sail the sea (23: 18–22)_
*   _He rained water from the sky which filled the channels in accordance with their capacity; now their torrents precipitated swelling foam similar to that which comes to the surface from the smelted ore when people manufacture ornaments and tools; this is how God distinguishes the truth from the falsehood. The scum is thrown leaving the useful material. This is how God strikes examples. (13: 17)_
*   _Do you observe the water you take? Is it you who rains it down from the cloud or We rain it? If We had determined, We could have made it brine. Now, why do you not express thanks? Do you observe the fire you kindle? Do you grow the tree used for it or we grow it? (56: 68–72)_
*   _Ask: Have you ever visualised that if all the water goes deep inside the earth who could have supplied you with running water? (67: 30)_

#### _Oceans and seas_

*   _He has let two kinds of Water Masses to meet; between them there is a connecting link, which they cannot disturb. Now, which of the functions of your Lord can you dispute? Pearls and corals come from both. (55: 19–21)_
*   _Nor the two streams of the seas are at the same level (in taste); the one is palatable; sweet and tasty to drink and the other one is salty and bitter; and from both of them you eat fresh and soft meat and extract material of ornaments for wearing; and you watch the ships that sail through them so that you people may find the Grace of God and be thankful. (35: 12)_

Qur’an tells us that:

1.  God has established a universal system, which runs without any disturbance due to highly precise nature of the laws that govern them and the properties of the matter, and God has also created a balance in the atmosphere of the Planet Earth. God created the universe, and the ultimate responsibility to sustain it is His, and He will decide its future in accordance with His Plan.
2.  God has created this equilibrium in a way that human beings cannot annihilate it. Nature has an inherent tendency to correct any disturbances that might destroy the equilibrium.
3.  Though human beings cannot annihilate this equilibrium altogether, they can certainly harm it within their reach.
4.  The natural disasters are often if not always of man’s own making; Nature only judges and takes corrective action resulting in what we know as natural disasters. These disasters are a way to correct the equilibrium and save the mankind from bigger disasters .
