#### By Muslim Mirror News (November 25, 2013)

![](https://javedjamilhome.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/09/8a9c5-0vxmbw15_erhuqzjp.jpg?w=736)  

New Delhi : “Allotment of 15 pc tickets for Lok Sabha will be the biggest test of the secular claims of the political parties”, said Dr Javed Jamil, the thinker and author of “Muslim Vision of Secular India: Destination & Roadmap” in a special interview telecast on ETV Urdu on Sunday in “Walk the talk” programme. He said that “Muslims are about 15 pc in India and it is their right to have 15 pc representation in all the democratic institutions of the country, and till a constitutional process is evolved to ensure their due representation, it is the duty of all the political parties that claim themselves to be secular to give 15 pc tickets to Muslims.” He further said that the political parties must also allocate 15 pc seats in their quota in Rajya Sabha to Muslims and must also compensate in Rajya Sabha for any shortage in Lok Sabha. The interview focused on the Muslim Agenda 2014 that Dr Jamil and Mr Sirajuddin Qureshi have posted to the chief functionaries of all the political parties of the country. Dr Jamil told that on the initiative of Mr Qureshi, he has culled some innovative proposals from his Approach Paper that can have a bearing on the Agenda for the coming elections. These are in addition to the demands that are already being debated.

Dr Jamil said that ”Muslims in India are constitutionally a minority, ideologically the second largest majority and socially part of the deprived majority, and they must work keeping in mind all the three statuses”. He told that the Agenda does not only include specific proposals for Muslims but also several important proposals that would empower the masses of the whole country. These included “introduction of Economic Disparity Index, Right to Health, Right to Justice, Right to Equal Opportunities and a paradigm shift in the tax policy.” Among proposals for the empowerment of Muslims, the Agenda emphasized the importance of allocation of 20 pc of funds for NGOs to Muslim NGOs. The Agenda also demanded setting up of 5 Muslim universities, one Muslim woman university, one Muslim sports college and a University for Applied Islamics. It also demanded reservation of Muslims in jobs according to a formula acceptable to Muslims.

When Dr Jamil was asked if he would support BJP if it accepts the Agenda, he said that “the Muslim Community and not he will decide who to vote for once different political parties make their positions known.” On being repeatedly asked, he refused to tell his preference and insisted that “he wanted to focus on the issues instead of talking about the merits and demerits of political parties.” He however said that no political party of the country has done enough to become a natural claimant of Muslim votes. Dr Jamil said that there was a need to set up a national level semi-political organization which can look after all the affairs of the community in a professional and systematic manner.
