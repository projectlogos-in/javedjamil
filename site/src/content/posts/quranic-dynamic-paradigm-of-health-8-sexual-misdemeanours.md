### **Sexual Misdemeanors**

God has been extremely kind to women; in every part of their biological functions, they have been assured of men’s support. Obviously, sex with multiple partners is mainly responsible for the spread of the STDs; and the chances are typically extremely low in sex partners having long term loyalty with each other. Some STIs can also be contracted by using [IV drug](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Drug_injection "Drug injection") [needles](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hypodermic_needle "Hypodermic needle") after their use by an infected person, as well as through any incident involving the contact of a wound with contaminated blood or through [childbirth](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Childbirth "Childbirth") or [breastfeeding](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Breastfeeding "Breastfeeding").
