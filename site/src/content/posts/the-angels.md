#### A Systematic Study of the Holy Qur’an

![](https://javedjamilhome.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/09/ec252-0yqgontx2vsrjmapw.jpg?w=736)  

Though God is absolutely self-sufficient and does not need assistance from any of His creations, it is in fitness of things for His majesty and Glory to own servants that are truly obedient to Him and carry out His commands with utmost efficiency and swiftness. These special servants, who possess no desire or will of their own, are called Angels. These mysterious creatures, common to almost all religions of the world, are beyond the capacity of human intellect to fully comprehend. They are said to have been created from the “light” indicating that they are special creatures, perhaps in the form of waves; these waves ought to be outside the spectrum visible to human eyes or audible to human ears. They are innumerable in number, appointed for hosts of functions; out of them four have special status — Jibril (Gabriel), Michael, Israfil and Izrael. Jibril has been the privileged carrier of God’s messages to the Prophets; Michael is said to have been appointed on the control of winds; Israfil will blow the Trumpet on the Doomsday and on the Resurrection Day; and Izrael is the angel of death.

Angels in Islam have resemblance to _devtas_ of Hindu mythology; but unlike _devtas_, angels are regarded true servants of God, their power being only by the will of God. It is not they but God who alone deserves glory and worship.

### **ANGELS IN THE QUR’AN**

> **In the name of God, the Kind, the Merciful**

Acknowledgment is in respect for God who differentiated the Earth and the Cosmic Tiers, and formed angels, ambassadors with wings, two, three or four. (35: 1)

(The angels say:) “Moreover, we do not come down except in accordance with the Law of your Lord. He owns what is before us (past), what is after (future) and what is in between (present). And your Lord does never forget anything. (19: 64)

He sends down angels with Spirit, which is a derivative of His Law, and lets them travel to the one chosen from among his devotees (with the message): Give warning that there is no god except Me; so be careful. (16 :2)

Say to whoever has been hostile towards Gabriel: it is surely he who brings down to your heart, when he gets the signal from God, an authentication of what (was dispatched) before, a Guide and Promise of Reward for the People of Commitment. Whoever has been hostile to God, His Executive (Angels), His Ambassadors and Gabriel and Michael (should know that) God is hostile to the Dissenters. (2: 97–98)

Surely, there have been put in charge over you (executives) in order to protect you: the Dignified Recorders; they (instantly) know whatever you do. (82: 10–12)

(This is through a process by which) the two Account-Keepers placed on the right and the left capture everything (in their records). Not a single statement does one make and a relentless invigilator in the close vicinity is ready to take note of it. (50: 17–18/A)

Surely, We do not dispatch the Executive-angels except with the Mandate; if they act without it, they would not be tolerated. (15: 8)

The Executive-angels and the Spirit travel up towards Him in the relative time of a day which is equivalent to your fifty thousand years. (70: 4)

By the total destroyers; by the joyful entertainers, by the speedy movers, the fierce competitors; the programmers of action; there will be a day when there will be a severe (first) blast, followed by another one; at that time their hearts will tremble; their eyes will feel humbled; they will ask (one another): Will we be able to return to our former state (of dominance)?; will we become like a decayed skeleton? They will say: if this happens this would be an irreparable loss. It will be one big loud happening; which bring them down to the surface. (79: 1–14)

#### Other translation

By the ones that destroy with violence, by the ones that act smartly, by the ones that move speedily! Now they move, one ahead of the other; now they execute the command. On that day something will be shaken; and there will follow the second blast. On that day hearts will be throbbing; and the eyes will be scary. They will discuss, “Will we be pushed back to our former state now? What! Will we become destroyed bones? They will say that it would be a return with huge loss. Now just this single blast will bring them on the surface (of the earth). (79:1–14)

The Cosmic Tiers almost totally break up from their upper part; and the executive-angels move along with the (Motion of) Acknowledgment of their Lord, and seek reprieve for the inhabitants of the Earth. (42: 5)

And there (will come) a time when the Cosmic Tier will split and the executive-angels will come down in a quick descent. The State at that stage will be governed in accordance with the Mandate for the sake of Kind Lord; and the day will be extremely difficult for the Dissenters. A stage (will come) when the perpetrator of injustice will bite his hand, exclaiming: “Oh I wish I had caught up with the Ambassador in travelling the road! Oh! I wish I had not taken that person as a friend. He had been diverting me from the Memorandum after it had come to me. Satan is (indeed) a deceiver of man.” (25: 25–29)
