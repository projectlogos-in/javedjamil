There is a popular Indian proverb: _“Ulta chor, kotwal ko daante”_ (Thief scolding the inspector). The proverb applies to all of West, but if it is applicable most to anyone, it is Donald Trump’s Republican Party. It is this party which is primarily responsible for the majority of world violence in last 50 years. But when for people, only a certain category of violence, “their violence” becomes bad and not “our violence” what else can we expect other than what Trump has called for. Trump forgets that violence in America and America-led or America-instigated violence in other parts of the world is killing more people very day than the number killed in California.

In America itself, mass shootings are taking place almost every day. According to a Guardian report, entailed, “1,052 mass shootings in 1,066 days”:

_“We have a pattern now of mass shootings in this country that has no parallel anywhere else in the world,” Barack Obama said on Wednesday._

_Data compiled by the crowd-sourced website_ [_ShootingTracker.com_](http://www.shootingtracker.com/) _reveals a shocking human toll: there is a mass shooting — defined as four or more people shot in one incident — nearly every day. (_Updated on 3 Dec 2015)”

[http://www.theguardian.com/us-news/2015/dec/02/mass-shootings-in-america-numbers-more-frequent-more-deadly](http://www.theguardian.com/us-news/2015/dec/02/mass-shootings-in-america-numbers-more-frequent-more-deadly)

California shooting was 353rd in 2015 alone, with 468 dead and 1312 injured, according to Dallas. But out of these 353, Trump found only one worthy of such condemnation that can mean in the long run ouster of all Muslims from America. And of course mass shooting deaths are only a few per cent of the total number of murders in America, which remains among the top nations of the world in terms of murder rates. According to another report,

_“More than 32,000 people have been killed and more than 67,000 injured by firearms each recent year in America.”_

And its overall record is horrible. West has been responsible for most of the violence in the last century. Despite its prosperity and power, West has been responsible for

• Most of the wars in the last century, most of deaths in those wars, consuming more than 160 million lives

• Most invasions; including colonization at a massive scale;

• Most attacks on countries including attacks on civilians;

• The only nuclear attacks made on the surface of the earth;

• Destruction of aborigines in big numbers;

• Most deaths in Muslim countries including more than 3 million since the beginning of the new century;

Western system has also been responsible for

• Huge increase in business related to sex, alcohol, gambling and organised violence; with huge effects on health, family and society;

• Maximum damage to environment, causing massive damage; and

• stockpiling of weapons of massive destruction.

The pains of the people killed in Iraq, Afghanistan and Syria by American bombardments or American weapons have no effect on the sensitivities of Obamas and Trumps. The grief of Palestinians does not bother them. They have no answer to the question: If they are always ready to avenge their deaths, how should Muslims avenge deaths of 3 million innocents in Iraq and other Muslim countries. Obama wants Muslims to condemn ISIS more strongly than they have done so far. But why should Muslims not condemn all the categories of violence and their perpetrators in proportion to their magnitude? If ISIS, Al-Qaedah and other Muslim organisations must be condemned one hundred thousand times for killing as many innocents — as reported in the international media — why should Muslims not condemn America 3.5 million times for killing as many innocents? Muslims have never been reluctant in condemning the Muslim terrorists, and Islamic scholars have gone to the extent of banishing them from Islam. But what about Americans and other Western thinkers and politicians? Will they condemn America and its allies for their murderous assaults on Muslim countries? Will they demand total Western disengagement from the Muslim world? And if Trump can ask for Muslims not to be allowed to travel to America, why Muslims all over the world should not demand total ban on West and Westernism from Muslim World?
