A world, which boasts of giving rights to promiscuous, prostitutes and perverts of all kinds in the name of “freedom of choice”, can only hope to reach the zenith of immorality at some point of time even in politics. “Democracy” after all is nothing but a license to the depraved, corrupt and selfish to capture power. The good and the morally upright find hardly any opportunity to even come close to power.
