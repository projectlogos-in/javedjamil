## Health as Process, Not State

If health is a process rather than a state, then every social and economic policy is, whether it intends to be or not, a health policy. A tax code, a licensing regime for alcohol, a labour law — each either sustains the process or degrades it.

This reframing does the practical work: it gives a single, measurable criterion — does this policy sustain the process of health, or does it not — that can be applied across domains usually kept separate from medicine entirely.
