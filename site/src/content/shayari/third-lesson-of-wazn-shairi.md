1.  Once the mathematical wazn of different words is clear, it is not difficult to fit them into different bahrs.
2.  It is to be noted that in every misra, same sequence of 2 and one has to be followed;
3.  Normally a 2 cannot be broken into 11, but in certain bahrs this is allowed, but it is done only when there are at least 3 2s in continuation; and in that case too only the middle of the three 2s can be broken. I will give its examples with bahrs.
4.  I will now introduce some of the common bahrs:

#### A. 2221 bahrs

In these bahrs, same combination of one and 2 thrice is repeated several times in the verse. For example, in the misra I had given, “Rafta Rafta who meree hastee ka saamaaN ho gaye, the bahr is 212221222122212. Now ever misra has to follow the same sequence.

Another shair in a direct 1222 bahr is:

jo KHan-jar aa-s-teeN kaa hai ba-hut kam zar-f ho-taa hai.  
1 2 2 2 1 2 2 2 1 2 2 2 1 2 2 2  
ka-haaN se hau-s-la aa-ye ke khul kar samne aaye  
1 2 2 2 1 2 2 2 1 2 2 21 2 2 2

#### B. 122 bahr

Similarly there can be a bahr in the twice 2 and then once 1 combination. See one shair of mine:

Lab hu-ey mun-ja-mid un-ke aa-ney ke baa-d  
2 1 2 2 1 2 2 1 2 2 1 2 (d here is at the end of the misra, so this 1 rukn will not be counted)  
Un-se baa-teiN hu-eeN un-ke jaa-ney ke baa-d  
2 1 2 2 1 2 2 1 2 2 1 2

similarly there isa bahr with one 1followed by one 2: 21212121212.   
HoN-t key shi-gaa-f bhar di-yey ga-yey  
2 1 2 1 2 1 2 1 2 1 2.  
Laf-z paa-ya-maa-l kar di-yey ga-yey  
2 1 2 1 2 1 2 1 2 1 2.

#### C. Another popular bahr (2 2121211221212)

See my own shair in this bahr:  
Hum-ne cha-haa-r sam-t ka-boo-tar u-ra di-yey  
2 2 1 2 1 2 1 1 2 2 1 2 1 2  
Aur yeh sa-majh ke bai-TH ga-yey am-n ho-gayaa

Note that here aur is used as 2 (or) and not as 21(au-r), which is also right.

There is huge number of ghazals in this bahr. Try to find out.

#### D. Similarly another bahr is 12121122121222

(Note that in this bahr the underlined 2 can be divided into 11. I will give the example of both. First see my shair in the first pattern

ye aa-d-mee ne hi to aa-d-mee se chhee-na hai  
1 2 1 2 1 1 2 2 1 2 1 2 2 2  
ki-see ka khaa-b ki-see ney ki-see se chhee-na hai

Now Faiz Ghazal where 2 is divided into 11:  
Gu-loN meiN raN-g bha-rey baa-de-nau ba-haa-r cha-le  
1 2 1 2 1 1 2 2 1 2 1 2 1 1 2
