There is another extremely popular bahr:  
21221122112222

Lab pe aa-tee hai du-aa ban-ke ta-man-naa mey-ree  
2 1 2 2 1 1 2 2 1 1 2 2 2 2  
The underlined and bold 2 can be divided into 11. See:  
Baa-t kar-nee mu-jhe mush-kil ka-bhi ai-see to na thee  
2 1 2 2 1 1 2 2 1 1 2 2 1 1 2  
jai-si ab ho gayi mush-kil ka-bhi ai-see to na thee.

A shair of mine in this bahr :  
Jis-m pe sak-ta sa taa-ree tha ni-gaa-heiN bey-ho-sh  
Us-ko de-khaa to la-gaa taa-j-ma-hal de-kh li-yaa

Ghalib’s famous ghazal is also in the same bahr:

Kau-n jee-taa hai te-ree zul-f je sar ho-ne tak  
aa-h ko chaa-hi-ye ek um-r a-sar ho-ney tak

Note that in the first misra, sh in the last equaling 1 is extra, which as already explained, is allowed.
