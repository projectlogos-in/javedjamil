#### **Mathematical Method by Dr Javed Jamil**

![](https://javedjamilhome.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/09/a7769-0ovfw5cxevtlr5fpe.jpg?w=736)  

1.  Lafz TaKHtee naheen taqtee hai: ta qaaf twa ya ain), tukre karne ke maane mein
2.  All the words have to be pronounced in their correct or accepted forms and cannot be deformed to fit the wazn
3.  all parts with two huroof with zabar, zair or paish on first and saakin on the other have the wazn of two like jab, kab, kis, lab, sun, din, gul, GHam, ghar,
4.  gha, pha, bha, dha, jha, kha, etc will be treated as single hurf
5.  if the second hurf is alif, waaw, chhoti ha and yaa, and they are in the middle of a word, they will always be counted as full harf and the parts having them will have wazn of 2 not one.
6.  All parts having just one harf with zabar, zair, paish or jazm will have a wazn of 1: di in diyaa, pa in palat gi in giraa, ja in jabal, Gh in Ghazal, si in sitam, ta in raat and baat, na in maan and shaan, etc.
7.  noon ghunna and hamza normally have no wazn. So wazn of maa and maaN are same, 2. not 21. the wazn of bar-baa-di-yaaN is 2212 and not 22121.
8.  see the wazn of following words for practice: KHaamosh (221), KHaamoshiyaan (2212), madhosh (221), mahnat (22), mu-laa-qaa-t (1221), ta-man-naa (122), mu-hab-bat (122), mu-sal-sal (122), ja-maa-l (121),
9.  alif, waav, yaa, chhotee ha at the end of a word can be taken as hidden, especially in words like hee, bhee, to, hai, ga, gee, samjha, gaya, etc. these, for example, hai can be used as 1 and 2 both. But this hiding should not be used in nouns and adjectives, particularly the words originating in Arabic and Faarsi. For example, the wazn of tan-haa should always be 22 and not 21, though shoraa often make this kind of mistakes.
10.  Now, those interested must rehearse the wazn of as many words as possible before I come up with the next lesson.
11.  If any brother can convert this lesson into urdu KHat, it will be great.
