Rooted in the Qurʾānic Paradigm of Health, the book treats health as a foundational objective of the Qur’anic worldview rather than merely one of its outcomes. Dr. Javed Jamil presents a distinctive framework that contrasts the Qur’anic approach with contemporary health paradigms, arguing that ethical, social, and lifestyle principles are integral to human well-being. Drawing on discussions around global public health challenges, including the COVID-19 pandemic, the book examines the relationship between health, morality, economics, and policy, offering a thought-provoking perspective on building healthier societies through a holistic and value-based approach.
