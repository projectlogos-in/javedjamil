Contemporary Sciences in the Light of Qurʾān examines the relationship between the Qur’anic worldview and contemporary scientific disciplines. Through an interdisciplinary approach, Dr. Javed Jamil explores medicine, biology, environmental studies, ethics, and the social sciences, presenting a framework that integrates scientific inquiry with Qur’anic principles. Intended for scholars, students, and general readers, the book contributes to ongoing discussions on science, religion, and human knowledge.
