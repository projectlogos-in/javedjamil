Cancer Preventive Measures in Islam: Let the World Benefit! reveals how following Islamic lifestyle guidelines may help reduce cancer risk. Discover the unique health benefits of practices like regular ablution (wudū), fasting, circumcision, longer breastfeeding, and Prophetic dietary habits. The book explores how restrictions on alcohol, narcotics, and unhealthy sexual practices in Islam offer protection against many diseases, including cancers. Supported by facts and figures, this insightful guide shows how integrating these principles can promote overall well-being and disease prevention.
