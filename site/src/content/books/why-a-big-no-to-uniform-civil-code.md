Why a Big No to Uniform Civil Code presents a critical examination of the proposed Uniform Civil Code from constitutional, social, and Islamic perspectives. Drawing on extensive research, legal analysis, and empirical evidence, the book argues that the debate over the UCC extends beyond the concerns of any single community and raises broader questions about pluralism, diversity, and social harmony in India. It explores key aspects of Islamic family law — including women’s rights, marriage, divorce, inheritance, and mehr — while comparing different legal and social frameworks. Offering a reasoned and evidence-based perspective, the book contributes meaningfully to one of the most significant public debates in contemporary India.
