Economics First or Health First? challenges the conventional belief that economic growth should take precedence over public health. Introducing the innovative concept of the Dynamic Paradigm of Health, the book argues that true development is possible only when health, ethics, and economics are integrated into a unified framework. Blending economics, public health, social policy, and ethical analysis, it offers a thought-provoking alternative to prevailing development models and presents practical ideas for building healthier, more just, and sustainable societies. A valuable read for policymakers, economists, healthcare professionals, researchers, and socially conscious readers.
