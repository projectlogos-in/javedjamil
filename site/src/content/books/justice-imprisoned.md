Justice Imprisoned examines the growing crisis of justice in the modern world, questioning the effectiveness of contemporary legal and institutional systems in delivering peace, equality, and human dignity. Despite vast investments in law enforcement and judicial mechanisms, crime, violence, and social injustice continue to rise. Dr. Javed Jamil offers a critical analysis of the structural failures that undermine justice, arguing that it has become captive to the very forces meant to protect it. The book presents a thought-provoking exploration of the relationship between justice, governance, and the future of civilized society.
