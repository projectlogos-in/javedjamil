Muslim Vision of Secular India: Destination & Roadmap presents a comprehensive framework for the socio-economic, educational, and political empowerment of Indian Muslims within the principles of Islam and the framework of the Indian Constitution. Dr. Javed Jamil critically examines contemporary challenges facing the community while proposing a holistic roadmap for inclusive national development, social justice, and ethical progress. Covering issues ranging from education, employment, healthcare, media, and finance to governance and community development, the book offers a thoughtful vision for strengthening both the Muslim community and India’s democratic and pluralistic future.
