The ideology that gives predominance to economics over all other spheres of life, without regard for its effects on health, family peace, or social order. Dr. Jamil has waged what he describes as a "ruthless war of attrition" against this ordering — arguing that when profit sets the terms, health, family, and social cohesion are treated as expendable.
